## FAQ (Shop FAQ section)

- **Can I edit the files?** Yes. Word files open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and Pages.
  Spreadsheets open in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers. The PDFs are fillable.
- **Do I need Canva?** No. Nothing needs a paid app.
- **What about the fonts?** The designs use Cormorant Garamond and Jost, free from Google Fonts.
  Google Docs has them built in.
- **Letter or A4?** Most PDFs come in both.
- **Are the forms legally binding?** They're professional templates, not legal advice. Have them
  reviewed for your state before you use them.
- **Can I use them at more than one location?** One purchase covers one business. Message us about
  more locations.

## Settings to choose

- **Etsy Payments:** on (required). Payouts go to Evelyn's bank account.
- **Offsite Ads:** Etsy turns these on for new shops. Under $10,000 a year in sales you can opt out;
  if you keep them, Etsy takes 15% of sales that come from those ads, and nothing otherwise. Keep them on
  for the first months: the exposure matters more than the fee right now.
- **Etsy Ads (onsite):** optional. Try $1–$2 a day on the bundle and the intake kit after the first
  few reviews.
- **Vacation mode:** never needed for digital downloads.
- **Personalization:** off.
- **Digital listings:** each listing is set to "Digital files", "I did", "A finished product" (made to
  order), so no shipping settings are needed.
- **AI disclosure:** Etsy asks sellers to be open about AI-assisted work. Every listing description
  says the products were made with the help of AI writing and design tools, reviewed and edited by hand.
  Evelyn's review (REVIEW.md) is what makes that true, so finish it before publishing.
