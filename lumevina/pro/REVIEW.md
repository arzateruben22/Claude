# Evelyn's review, before anything goes live

Every product was written from Lumevina's own forms and general esthetics practice, with AI help.
The listings say a licensed esthetician reviewed and edited them, so this review is what makes that
true. It takes about two hours. Open each file in `dist/`, read it as if a client or a fellow
esthetician were holding it, and change anything that isn't how you'd do it.

Mark each box when you're happy. Edits go into the `src/` files (or send them over and they'll be
made), then `python3 build.py` rebuilds everything, photos included.

## 01 · Intake & Consent Kit
- [ ] Health History questions: anything you ask that's missing, or anything you'd never ask?
- [ ] Isotretinoin: 12 months before waxing, peels and dermaplaning. Is that your rule, or 6?
- [ ] Retinoids: told about or stopped 7 days before peels, dermaplaning and waxing. Your number?
- [ ] Chemical Peel Consent: the risks, the 48-hour and 7-day rules, SPF for 2 weeks.
- [ ] Waxing Consent: the areas list, and the Brazilian wording.
- [ ] Studio Policies: $25 deposit, 24 hours' notice, 50% late fee, 15 minutes late. These are
      examples for buyers, but they should be sensible.
- [ ] Photo Release: the three choices.

## 02 · Aftercare Cards
- [ ] Every timing on all 8 cards (24 hours, 48 hours, 7 days, 3 to 5 weeks and so on).
- [ ] Body Waxing: "skip intimacy for 24 hours" after a bikini or Brazilian. Keep or change?
- [ ] Lash Lift and Brow Lamination: how long results last, how long to keep dry.
- [ ] Anything clients always ask you that a card should answer.

## 03 · Skin Analysis & Treatment Record
- [ ] The face map zones and the marking key are the ones you'd use.
- [ ] The concerns list and the Glogau labels.

## 04 · Service Menu & Price List
- [ ] The sample services and prices look realistic for a studio buyer (they're placeholders).
- [ ] The 40 descriptions sound like a real esthetician wrote them. Rewrite any that don't.
- [ ] "Say this, not this": the cosmetic-versus-medical wording.

## 05 · Membership Pricing Calculator
- [ ] Open it in Excel or Google Sheets and change a few blue numbers. Do the answers make sense?

## 06 · Bookkeeping & Profit Tracker

## 07 · Scripts
