One evening

Set it up

  1. Make a free Pirate Ship account at pirateship.com with the Lumevina email, and add a card. It's charged only when you buy a label.
  2. Use the studio as the "Ship from" address, not home. It prints on every label, and anything returned goes back there.
  3. Turn on email notifications in Pirate Ship's settings, with the Lumevina name and logo. Each client then gets their tracking number by email, automatically.
  4. Set up the printer: plug it in, load the labels, and install MUNBYN's free driver from munbyn.com (there's one for Mac and one for Windows). On the new laptop, install it again; it takes about five minutes.
  5. Print a test label. In Pirate Ship, set the label size to 4 × 6 (thermal printer), then print one.
  6. Make a packing spot: the scale, a bin with mailers and boxes, the bags, tape and paper towels, and the printer within reach.
  7. Practice once. On the dashboard, under Shipping, press Download for Pirate Ship (it has sample orders until real ones come in). In Pirate Ship choose Import, then Upload a spreadsheet, and pick that file. Look, but don't buy.

Skincare is liquid

Pack it so nothing leaks

  1. Tighten every cap and run a strip of tape around it.
  2. Bag each bottle in its own zip bag, with a folded paper towel inside.
  3. Wrap glass, pumps and droppers in one layer of bubble wrap, and send them in a box, never a mailer.
  4. Fill the gaps with tissue or bubble wrap until nothing rattles when you shake it.
  5. Put the packing slip on top, close it, and tape along every seam.

The routine

Every order, about ten minutes

  1. Open the dashboard, then Shipping. Everything to pack is listed with its package, weight and ship-by date.
  2. Press Packing slips and print them on the label printer: one 4 × 6 slip per order, with a thank-you note.
  3. Pack each order, weigh it, and fix the ounces on the dashboard if they're off.
  4. Press Download for Pirate Ship. In Pirate Ship: Import, Upload a spreadsheet, pick the file, check the prices, and buy. Pirate Ship shows what each USPS option costs for every package; take the cheapest.
  5. Print the labels and stick one on each package.
  6. Mark each one shipped on the dashboard, with the tracking number and what the label cost. The books then use the real postage.
  7. Hand them off: schedule a free pickup at usps.com the night before, and the carrier takes them from the door. Or drop them at the post office, or in a blue mailbox if they fit.

The promise on the website is "ships in 1–2 business days". A look at the dashboard each workday morning keeps it: anything under To pack goes out that day or the next. Glow Routine boxes wait under Coming up until three days before their date.

Good to know

Before the first package

  • Pickup is the cheapest shippingA client with an appointment in the next 45 days gets "Pick up at my visit" picked for them at checkout. It's free for them and costs no postage.
  • Coverage is built inGround Advantage and Priority Mail include up to $100 of insurance, enough for any order on the shelf. If something arrives broken, ask for a photo and send a replacement.
  • Flammable or aerosol?If a label says flammable, or it's an aerosol spray, it can only go by ground and needs special marking. Check before mailing it. Cleansers, serums, creams and SPF ship normally.
  • Ask each brand firstBefore shipping a brand's products, check with the rep that a professional account allows selling online and shipping to clients. Some professional-only lines don't.
  • Sales tax on productsProducts sold to California addresses need sales tax collected. Ask the accountant before the first shipped sale, so it's set up from the start.
  • New laptopPirate Ship works in any browser, Mac or Windows, so there's nothing to move. Only the printer driver needs installing again.
