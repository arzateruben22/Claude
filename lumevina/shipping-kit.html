2. Set up once

One evening.

  1. Make a free Pirate Ship account with the Lumevina email and add a card. It's charged only when you buy a label. Use the studio as the "Ship from" address.
  2. In Pirate Ship's settings, turn on email notifications. Each client then gets their tracking number by email, automatically.
  3. Plug in the printer, load the labels, and install the free driver from munbyn.com. In Pirate Ship, set the label size to 4 × 6 and print a test label.
  4. Practice once: in your dashboard → Shipping, press Download for Pirate Ship, then in Pirate Ship choose Import → Upload a spreadsheet. Look, but don't buy.

3. Every order

About ten minutes. The website promises it ships in 1–2 business days.

  1. Open your dashboard → Shipping. Each order shows its package, weight and ship-by date. Press Packing slips and print them.
  2. Pack each order (see below) and weigh it.
  3. Press Download for Pirate Ship, import it in Pirate Ship, buy the cheapest USPS label, then print and stick it on.
  4. Mark it shipped on the dashboard, with the tracking number.
  5. Schedule a free USPS pickup the night before, or drop it at the post office.

4. Pack it so nothing leaks

  • Tighten every cap and run a strip of tape around it.
  • One bottle per zip bag, with a folded paper towel inside.
  • Glass, pumps and droppers: one layer of bubble wrap, in a box, never a mailer.
  • Fill the gaps until nothing rattles, put the packing slip on top, and tape every seam.

6. Good to know

  • Pickup is the cheapest shipping. A client with a visit coming up gets "Pick up at my visit" picked at checkout: free for them, no postage for you.
  • Breakage is covered. USPS Ground Advantage includes up to $100 of insurance. If something arrives broken, ask for a photo and send a replacement.
  • No aerosols or anything labeled flammable without checking first. Cleansers, serums, creams and SPF ship normally.
