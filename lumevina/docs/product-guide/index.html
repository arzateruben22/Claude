Before you start

A few things first

Each product has its own page: when to use it, how, what's normal, and the signs that mean stop and message Evelyn. Your routine is the one Evelyn built for you; if something here and her advice to you differ, follow her.

Your routine, in order

Morning: cleanser, toner, serum, eye gel, moisturizer, SPF. Night: cleanse (double cleanse if you wore makeup or SPF), toner, your treatment product (your active or acne med), serum, eye gel, moisturizer. Thin to thick, and a spot treatment goes on last, only on the spot. Please be sure to wash hands before applying eye products to prevent actives from bleeding into eye area and causing dryness or irritation.

Ask Evelyn before you start anything if

You have, or are on:

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Accutane, past or present
  • Prescription retinoids or antibiotics
  • Any other prescription
  • A reaction, rash, burning or swelling
  • Eczema, rosacea, psoriasis or another condition

What not to mix

Not on the same night: a retinoid with an acid; two exfoliants; benzoyl peroxide with retinol. Vitamin C and Mandelic Acid goes in the morning, actives at night. Nothing active on skin that's freshly treated or waxed. Niacinamide and vitamin C are fine together.

Sunscreen

Every morning, rain or shine, SPF 30 or higher, enough of it, and reapplied when you're outside. It's the one product that protects everything else we're doing: every active makes you burn more easily, and sun is what brings dark spots back.

Starting a mandelic serum

Start with the strength I give you, usually the lowest, a few nights a week, and build up to nightly as your skin allows. Moving up a strength is my call, after a few weeks with no stinging or flaking. If it ever burns or your skin gets sensitive, back off and message me.

Evelyn to confirm

Morning or night? Your routines and "what not to mix" put the mandelic serums in the morning; your answer on starting them says a few nights a week.

Retinal or retinol

Retinol is the gentler, slower one and where most people start; retinal works faster and is for skin that's already used to a retinoid or wants more. Either way: a pea for the whole face, 2 or 3 nights a week at first, moisturizer over it, SPF every morning, and never alongside an acid on the same night.

Choosing and applying sunscreen

Broad spectrum, SPF 30 or higher, every morning, enough of it (about 2 thin fingers worth), reapplied if you're outside or in a room with windows. Mineral if your skin is sensitive, acne-prone or freshly treated. The ones I carry are the ones I trust; ask me which fits you.

Before a treatment

Pause retinol or any retinoid, acids (glycolic, lactic, salicylic, mandelic), scrubs and at-home peels for 3 to 5 days before your facial; a prescription retinoid like tretinoin, about a week, and tell me you're on it. Keep your cleanser, moisturizer and SPF as usual. Be sure to at least apply moisturizer and SPF the day of your appointment.

After a treatment

After a facial, 2 or 3 days. After a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling, about a week, once the skin has stopped flaking and feels normal again. Then start back at a few nights a week, not every night.

Running low, or skipped a few days

Don't stretch it or stop; message me and I'll set one aside for pickup, or order it from the shop with shipping. Running out of the treatment product for a week or two usually means starting back at a few nights a week.

No, a few days doesn't undo anything. Pick it back up at the same strength. If it's been a week or more, go back to a few nights a week with your actives first, then build up again. Reach out if you're unsure.

Using your own products too

Usually yes; what matters is not doubling up: two exfoliants, two retinoids or several actives at once. Send me a photo of what you're using and I'll tell you what stays, what goes and what waits.
