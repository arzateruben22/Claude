A plan in three stages

From one chair to a Lumevina house.

Prepared for Evelyn Romero · Woodland Hills, CA

The problem

Your income stops when your hands stop.

Every dollar Lumevina earns today needs you in the treatment room. Fully booked and never sick, that still caps out at roughly $200k–$230k a year before rent, product and taxes. More hours isn't a plan, and you already need help.

The idea

Earn more per hour, then earn from more hands.

First, raise what each hour in your chair earns and add money that doesn't need your hands at all. Then bring in a few young, licensed lash, brow and nail artists who have the talent but no business setup. They work under the Lumevina name, clients book them in the Lumevina app, and one Glow Rewards program ties everyone together. Their clients become your facial clients.

Stage 1 · Months 0–6

Grow your own chair

  • Glow Membership (monthly)
  • Add-ons at booking
  • Fill empty slots, refer a friend
  • Retail with auto-refill
Stage 2 · Months 6–12

Pilot: two artists

  • 1–2 licensed lash / brow artists
  • Booked through Lumevina
  • Shared Glow Rewards
  • Measure: do their clients book you?
Stage 3 · Year 2–3

The Lumevina Collective

  • 6–8 artists, one Lumevina space
  • Lumevina app in the App Store
  • Lumevina Academy (paid mentorship)
  • Each stage only if the last one worked

What it costs to start
  • Go live: Stripe (2.9% + 30¢ per payment), database ~$25/mo, texts ~$10–$30/mo
  • Attorney: one-hour consult $300–$600; artist agreements $1.5k–$5k
  • App Store: $99/year, at Stage 2
  • Bigger space: only at Stage 3, only after the pilot works
Risks, and how we handle them
  • Licensing: licensed artists only, working in a licensed space
  • Employee rules (AB5): artists set their own prices and are paid directly; attorney signs off first
  • Artists leaving: make staying worth more: clients, brand, points, space
  • Your time: Ruben runs operations; you set quality

The next 90 days
  1. Write down today's numbers from Acuity: bookings, repeat clients, no-shows, average ticket.
  2. Switch on live payments and move booking from Acuity to Lumevina.
  3. Launch Glow Membership and add-ons.
  4. One-hour attorney consult on how artists are set up.
  5. Recruit one licensed lash or brow artist and track how many of their clients book you.
Start small, prove it with one artist, and grow only on what works. The system is already built; this plan is how Lumevina outgrows one chair.
