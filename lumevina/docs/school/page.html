Questions

Good to know.

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to understand their skin, from a first real routine to a plan for acne, dark spots or aging. You don’t need to be a Lumevina client, and it’s even better if you are.

How long do I have access?

For life, including new lessons as Evelyn adds them. Each lesson is 7 to 12 minutes, so most people finish a level in a week or two.

Do I need to buy products?

No. The course works with what you already own and shows you what to keep. The kit is there if you’d like to start with products Evelyn trusts.

How does the 1-on-1 with Evelyn work?

After you join, you pick a time for a 45-minute video call. Evelyn looks at your skin, goes through your products and writes your routine. Thirty days later, a 15-minute check-in to fine-tune it. There are six spots a month.

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s skincare education from a licensed esthetician. Anything medical, like cystic acne, rashes, changing moles or prescriptions, belongs with a dermatologist or doctor, and the lessons tell you when.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days if you’ve finished fewer than a quarter of the lessons. Kits can be returned unopened.
