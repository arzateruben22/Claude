    FAQ & Policies

    Good to know, before you glow.

    Still wondering about something? Ask Evelyn.

    New here? What to book

    New Clients Start Here (from $220): a consultation and a facial built for your skin. For acne, start with the Acne Bootcamp's New Client Consultation + Treatment ($220).

    Hours

    Tuesday 12 to 7 PM; Wednesday and Thursday 11 AM to 7 PM; Friday 10 AM to 5 PM; Saturday 9:30 AM to 6 PM. Closed Sundays and Mondays. See openings in Book. After-hours visits are an extra $50; ask first.

    Deposits & payment

    Some treatments take a non-refundable deposit when you book (the menu shows each one). It holds your spot, goes toward your total, and with 48 hours' notice moves to a new date in the same month. Pay the rest at your visit: cash preferred, card with a fee, or Klarna, Affirm or Afterpay.

    Shipping, returns & the actives
    • Shipping is $8, free on orders over $75, or pick up free at your visit. Orders ship from Woodland Hills within two business days by USPS Ground Advantage, with tracking by email.
    • Returns: unopened products within 14 days for a refund. Opened skincare can't be returned. Anything damaged in transit is replaced: email a photo within a few days of delivery.
    • Some products need a quick consult first (the Acne Med, the 11% and 15% mandelic serums, the retinoids, Hydroxi). Tap Needs a quick consult on the product, tell Evelyn about your skin, and she unlocks it for your email, usually within a day, at no charge.
    • Not local? The online Skin Consult (15 minutes, $35) builds your routine and unlocks what you need; the $35 comes back as a code on your first order.
    Cancelling & rescheduling
    • Change or cancel at least 48 hours ahead, from the link in your confirmation.
    • Same-day changes: the deposit is kept, a $43 fee goes to the card on file, and rebooking takes a new deposit.
    • No-shows are charged 50% of the service and may not be able to rebook.
    • Saturdays can't be moved or cancelled. Moving one takes a new deposit; cancelling forfeits it and adds a fee.

    Need to reschedule?

    Late arrivals & refunds

    There's a 10-minute grace period; after that the appointment may be cancelled and the deposit lost. Services are non-refundable. If anything comes up after your visit, reach out within 24 hours. Please read each service description before booking.

    Before & after your facial
    • Stop retinoids 5–7 days before, and exfoliants (salicylic, glycolic, benzoyl peroxide, AHAs/BHAs) 2–3 days before.
    • No waxing or shaving your face 2 days before or after.
    • Botox, fillers or permanent makeup: wait 2 weeks (or have your facial first, the same day or a week before).
    • Accutane, recent laser, surgery, radiation or chemo: wait 6–12 months or bring physician clearance.
    • Sunburn, windburn, open wounds or a cold sore? Tell us so we can reschedule.
    • Afterwards: no makeup right away, and no workouts or heavy sweating for 24 hours (a week after a body peel). Avoid booking the day of a big event.
    Location & parking

    Woodland Hills, CA; the exact address is in your confirmation email. Park free in the lot, then wait in your car and text before coming up.

    Products, virtual visits & gift cards

    Order from the shelf anytime: shipping is $8 (free over $75), or pick up at your visit. Virtual consultations by video are $50, Mondays only; reach out first. The quick online Skin Consult (15 minutes, $35, credited to your first order) books from the shop and unlocks the actives that need Evelyn's yes. Gift certificates never expire, and anything left after a visit stays on them.
