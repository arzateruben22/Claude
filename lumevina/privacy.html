Privacy Policy

Lumevina Aesthetics Spa · Woodland Hills, California · Last updated October 2026

Lumevina Aesthetics Spa ("Lumevina," "we") provides skincare and waxing services, and this website and mobile app let you book appointments, shop her skincare, purchase gift certificates, and join Glow Membership and Glow Points. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Booking details — your name, email address, mobile number if you give one, the services you select, your appointment date and time, and your signed consent.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes directly to Stripe and is never stored on our servers. When you book or join a membership, Stripe keeps your card on file; we keep only Stripe's reference to it, its brand and last four digits, and a record of each amount paid.
  • Glow Points and membership — your points balance and activity, your referral code, your birthday month if you choose to share it, and your membership's plan, facials and billing dates.
  • Intake information — the forms you complete before treatments, used solely to provide your service safely.
  • Your skin profile, if you fill one in — your skin type, what you're working on, the products you use, supplements you take, treatments you've had, what your skin reacts to and your goal, in your own words. It's saved only when you tick the box to say yes.
  • What the chat remembers — when you're signed in and tell Ask Lumevina something lasting about your skin or routine ("I use a vitamin C serum"), it keeps a short note of it for your next chat. It doesn't keep medicines, prescriptions or health conditions; those stay on your pre-visit form, for Evelyn.
  • Shop orders — what you ordered, your shipping address if it ships, and the order number, so we can send it and tell you where it is. Signed in, your own past orders show under Buy again; nobody else's do.
  • Consult requests — when a product needs Evelyn's okay first, or you book the online Skin Consult: your name, email, the few lines you write about your skin, and the pre-visit form if you link it. Evelyn reads it, answers by email, and her yes is kept with your email so the product unlocks for you.
  • Questions you send through Ask Lumevina — the question, your name and email, and any photos you add. Photos are stored privately and only Evelyn sees them.

How we use it

  • To schedule and confirm your appointments and process deposits.
  • To charge the card on file, as the booking policy you agree to at booking describes: the rest of your visit when you choose to pay that way, a late cancellation or no-show fee, and your monthly membership.
  • To run Glow Points and Glow Membership — points, birthday bonuses, referrals, and banked facials.
  • To send booking confirmations and reminders by email, and, only if you ask for them, text reminders (message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to end them). Now and then we may email you a review request after a visit, a note when it's time for your next one, or a reminder when your skincare may be running low; each has a link to stop those emails. Appointment emails keep coming while you have a booking.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To make chat answers specific to you. Your skin profile and the chat's notes are used only in your own chats and by Evelyn, never shown to anyone else. Ask Lumevina doesn't advise on supplements; it passes those questions to Evelyn.
  • To answer chat questions. Ask Lumevina's everyday answers are written by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which also drafts the replies to questions sent to Evelyn, using any photos you added. Evelyn reads and edits every reply before it goes to you; the assistant never diagnoses.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share it with third parties except the service providers that make the product work (payment processing, email and text delivery, hosting, calendar sync, and the AI assistant that answers chat questions), each bound to use it only for that purpose.
  • We do not share your mobile number or text consent with anyone for their own marketing.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You can book as a guest without creating an account. Signed in, you can see, change or delete your skin profile and each of the chat's notes any time in My Lumevina. You may also ask us to correct or delete your information at any time — email us and we'll take care of it. Deleting your account removes your rewards balance and history.

Retention & security

We keep booking records only as long as needed for scheduling, accounting, and the policies described at booking. Photos you send with a question stay with that question, and we delete them whenever you ask. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to Lumevina.

Children

Our services and app are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
