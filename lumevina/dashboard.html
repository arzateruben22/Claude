    Client questions

    Ask Lumevina answers the everyday questions on the website and app, any time. Anything personal lands here with a drafted reply: check it, change anything, and send. Members are promised an answer within 24 hours, everyone else within 2 days.

        Settle visits

        After each appointment, record the balance paid in person — cash or card — or mark a no-show. This is what closes the invoice.

          Shipping

          Shelf orders, Glow Routine boxes and Skin School kits going out by mail. Pack each one in the package shown, make the labels in Pirate Ship from the download, then mark it shipped with the tracking number and what the label cost. The books then use the real postage.

                Bubble mailer, 6 × 10 in: one plastic bottle or tube. Small box, 6 × 6 × 4 in: two or three items, or anything glass, with a pump or a dropper. Medium box, 10 × 8 × 4 in: sets and kits. Every bottle goes in a zip bag with a folded paper towel. Postage shown is an estimate from USPS's 2026 prices; Pirate Ship shows the exact price before you buy.

                Bookkeeping & profit

                Everything that came in, what it cost you, and what you keep: services, membership dues, retail, gift certificates, card fees, product cost and the Founding Five kits.

                Card fees are estimated at 2.9% + 30¢ a charge (Stripe). A balance counts once you mark the visit paid under Settle visits. Buying retail stock isn't an expense here: its cost counts when a product sells or goes out in a Founding Five kit, so nothing is counted twice. Log the product you use in treatments as Treatment supplies.

                Glow Membership

                Monthly recurring revenue is what memberships bring in before the month starts. Facials owed are banked visits members have paid for and not yet used. With two waiting, a member isn't billed until they book one.

                Extra Mondays on the website

                Gift certificates

                Outstanding is money you've collected but not yet redeemed — a liability (service you still owe) until the code is used.

                Use a gift certificate

                At the visit, enter the code and what the visit comes to. The certificate covers what it can: if it's worth more, the rest stays on it for next time; if the visit costs more, the client pays only the difference.

                For your accountant: gift-card sales are a liability, not income, until redeemed. Bookkeeping above shows them as money in when sold (cash basis) on their own line, so your accountant can move them if Lumevina books them as a liability instead. Gifts members send from their plan aren't counted: those were paid for through dues.

                Skin School Live

                Classes booked on the Skin School page. They run on Sundays and Monday evenings, when the studio is closed, so they never take a facial slot. Kept is what's left after card fees and each guest's kit, journal and drinks.
