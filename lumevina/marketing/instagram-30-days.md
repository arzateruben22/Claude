# Instagram, 30 days, in her voice

One post or story a day, local, in Evelyn's voice: warm, short, first person, plain. Nothing here claims a result; the skin facts are from the general esthetics layer and her own answers, hedged. She swaps in her own photos and words wherever she wants; the Monday draft writes the next week in the same voice. Hashtags: pick five per post from the list at the end.
