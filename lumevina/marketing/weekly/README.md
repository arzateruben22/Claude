# Weekly drafts

Every Monday at 6:46 AM Pacific a scheduled task drafts the week here as `<date>.md`: five posts or stories in Evelyn's voice, two ad variants for each running Meta campaign, a Google Business Profile post, a read of any numbers dropped into `../results/`, and a line confirming the rules in MARKETING.md were followed. It pushes the file to the branch and sends Ruben a notification. Nothing is posted or spent by it; she posts what she likes.

To change its day, time or what it writes, say so in the Claude session that set it up, or edit it under scheduled tasks on claude.ai.
