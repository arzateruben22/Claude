# Local listings: where "facial near me" is decided

Free, and worth more than the ads in the first months. Paste-ready copy; she adds photos of the studio and herself.

## Google Business Profile

**Category:** Facial spa (primary), Esthetician, Skin care clinic, Waxing hair removal service.
**Name:** Lumevina Aesthetics Spa. **Area:** Woodland Hills, CA (service-area or address, her choice; the address is sent with confirmations).
**Hours:** Tue 12–7, Wed 11–7, Thu 11–7, Fri 10–5, Sat 9:30–6, closed Sun and Mon (the dashboard's hours are the source).
**Booking link:** `https://lumevina.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp#book`
**Description (750 chars max):**
> Lumevina Aesthetics Spa is Evelyn Romero's one-on-one skincare studio in Woodland Hills. Every treatment is with her: customized facials, the Ageless Grace facial with oxygen and a tightening booster, ProCell microchanneling, Refresh and Corrective peels, dermaplaning, an Acne Bootcamp with a home routine she adjusts with you, and waxing (Wax Wednesday: a Brazilian is $65). The professional skincare she uses, Face Reality, GlyMed Plus, Mixi and Circadia, is on her website: pick it up free at your visit, or have it shipped. Book online any time; new clients start with a consultation and a fully customized facial.

**Services to list** (name, price, one line): copy from `js/services.js`: every treatment with its price and her description. **Products to list:** every in-stock product with its photo, price and label line (the feed has them).

**Posts, weekly (the Monday draft writes them):** one "what's new" (a product back in stock, a Wednesday reminder), one offer (Wax Wednesday; the welcome perk, free pickup), one photo of the studio or a product in her hand. Each post: 150 to 300 characters, one photo, the booking link.

**Reviews:** the review request after each visit already goes out with her Google link; she replies to every review in a sentence (thank them by first name, nothing about their skin).

**Q&A to seed (she asks and answers them herself):** "Do I need to stop my products before a facial?" · "Is there parking?" · "Do you take new clients?" · "Can I buy the products without a visit?" The answers are in her playbook and the FAQ.

## Nextdoor

Claim the business page (free). Description: the Google one, shortened to 400 characters. First post: "Hi neighbors, I'm Evelyn, the esthetician behind Lumevina in Woodland Hills. Facials, peels, waxing, and the professional skincare I use is now on my website for free pickup. Book online any time." Then one post every two weeks: a Wax Wednesday reminder, a product back in stock, a seasonal tip in her words (from the playbook once she's answered). Nextdoor Ads, if ever: the `pickup` creative, 5-mile radius, $5/day.

## Yelp

Claim the free profile; categories Skin Care, Waxing; the booking link; hours; 10 photos. No paid Yelp ads to start: the reviews and the booking link do the work. Reply to every review.

## Apple Maps and Bing Places

Free; the same name, category, hours and link. Ten minutes each.

## Reserve with Google

Once the website booking is live (v3), Google can show a Book button on the Maps listing. It needs a booking partner integration; GlossGenius offered it. On the website side it's a later step; until then the booking link in the profile does the job.

## The one rule

Every listing says the same name, phone, hours and link. Mismatches cost rankings.
