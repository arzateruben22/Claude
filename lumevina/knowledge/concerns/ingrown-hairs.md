## What should they avoid?

## Habits and everyday tips you give

From day 2 or 3 after your wax, exfoliate the area gently 2 or 3 times a week and keep it moisturized. Loose cotton, especially after workouts. Regular waxing every 4 to 6 weeks, so the hair stays fine.

## When must this come to you, or to a doctor?

An ingrown that's painful, hot, pus-filled or keeps coming back in the same spot, or bumps that spread: message me with a photo. Anything that looks infected should be seen by a doctor.
