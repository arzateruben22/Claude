# Her products, in order

## The order of steps, morning and night

Morning: cleanser, toner, serum, eye gel, moisturizer, SPF. Night: cleanse (double cleanse if you wore makeup or SPF), toner, your treatment product (your active or acne med), serum, eye gel, moisturizer. Thin to thick, and a spot treatment goes on last, only on the spot. Please be sure to wash hands before applying eye products to prevent actives from bleeding into eye area and causing dryness or irritation.

## Starting the mandelic serums

Start with the strength I give you, usually the lowest, a few nights a week, and build up to nightly as your skin allows. Moving up a strength is my call, after a few weeks with no stinging or flaking. If it ever burns or your skin gets sensitive, back off and message me.

## Retinal B3 Serum vs. Retinol Peptide Serum

Retinol is the gentler, slower one and where most people start; retinal works faster and is for skin that's already used to a retinoid or wants more. Either way: a pea for the whole face, 2 or 3 nights a week at first, moisturizer over it, SPF every morning, and never alongside an acid on the same night.

## What not to mix, or not on the same night

Not on the same night: a retinoid with an acid; two exfoliants; benzoyl peroxide with retinol. Vitamin C and Mandelic Acid goes in the morning, actives at night. Nothing active on skin that's freshly treated or waxed. Niacinamide and vitamin C are fine together.

## Anything you always say about sunscreen

Every morning, rain or shine, SPF 30 or higher, enough of it, and reapplied when you're outside. It's the one product that protects everything else we're doing: every active makes you burn more easily, and sun is what brings dark spots back.

## When should a client stop a product and message you?

If anything is burning, stinging or feels sensitive, reach out to me right away so we can make changes. If your skin has eczema, dermatitis, psoriasis or any unusual reaction or flare-up, stop your actives immediately and message me.
