# Questions clients ask: Booking and the day itself

The exact questions clients text Evelyn, with her answers. Empty sections mean she hasn't answered yet: the assistant hands those questions to her.

## "Can I switch to a different treatment when I get there?"

Often yes, if the time allows and your skin is right for it; just tell me when you arrive or message me beforehand so I can plan. Switching to something longer isn't always possible on the day.

## "Can I add something on the day, like dermaplaning?"

Usually, there is always time for add ons. If it is microchanneling please let me know at least one day in advance.

## "Can I book with a friend, back to back?"

Yes, back to back works. Book two consecutive times on the calendar, or message me and I'll set them up together. Every treatment is one on one, so you'll go one after the other.

## "I don't know which facial to book. Which one is for me?"

If you're new and don't have acne concerns, book New Clients Start Here. If you're new with acne concerns, book the Acne Bootcamp consultation + treatment. Returning clients: the Lumevina Custom Facial, or the Monthly Acne Treatment if we're working on acne. Not sure? Message me and I'll point you to the right one.
