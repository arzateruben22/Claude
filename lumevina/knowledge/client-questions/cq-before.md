# Questions clients ask: Before a visit

The exact questions clients text Evelyn, with her answers. Empty sections mean she hasn't answered yet: the assistant hands those questions to her.

## "Do I need to stop my products before my facial? Which ones, and for how long?"

Yes please. Pause retinol or any retinoid, acids (glycolic, lactic, salicylic, mandelic), scrubs and at-home peels for 3 to 5 days before your facial; a prescription retinoid like tretinoin, about a week, and tell me you're on it. Keep your cleanser, moisturizer and SPF as usual. Be sure to at least apply moisturizer and SPF the day of your appointment.

## "Can I still come in if I'm on my period?"

Yes, you can come in. Skin can be a little more sensitive around your period, so a facial may feel more reactive and a wax a bit more tender, but nothing about it is off limits. For a Brazilian, a tampon or cup is fine.

## "I'm breaking out right now. Should I still come in?"

Please do. Breakouts are exactly what a treatment is for: I'll adjust what we do to calm them and clear what's ready. The only thing I won't work on is open, raw or infected skin, so if anything is weeping or very painful, send me a photo first.

## "How long does my hair need to be for a wax?"

About a quarter inch, the length of a grain of rice: roughly 2 to 3 weeks after shaving, or 4 to 6 weeks after your last wax. Too short and the wax can't grip it.

## "Should I shave or trim before my wax?"

No shaving, please; it resets the growth and makes the next wax patchier and more uncomfortable. If it's very long you can trim, but not shorter than a quarter inch. Exfoliate gently 2 or 3 days before and come with clean, dry skin and no lotion.

## "I'm going on vacation / I just got some sun. Can I still get my treatment?"

No treatment on sunburned or freshly tanned skin, and no sun or tanning for a day or two after a wax, peel or dermaplaning. For a trip, book at least 2 or 3 days before you leave, and I'll go gentler on the actives if you're going to be in the sun.

## "It's my first Brazilian. Any tips?"

Let the hair grow to a quarter inch, exfoliate a couple of days before, shower that day, and wear loose cotton. It takes about 30 to 40 minutes and the first one is the most sensitive; it gets easier every time. Tell me first about any medications, skin issues or pregnancy.
