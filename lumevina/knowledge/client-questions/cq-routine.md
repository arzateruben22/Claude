# Questions clients ask: Routines and products

The exact questions clients text Evelyn, with her answers. Empty sections mean she hasn't answered yet: the assistant hands those questions to her.

## "Can I keep using my own products with the ones you gave me?"

Usually yes; what matters is not doubling up: two exfoliants, two retinoids or several actives at once. Send me a photo of what you're using and I'll tell you what stays, what goes and what waits.

## "Do I need everything you recommended, or what should I start with?"

If you're working with a budget, we can absolutely start with the essentials: cleanser, hydrator, moisturizer, and SPF**, then gradually introduce additional products as you're ready. Just keep in mind that this approach may slow down your progress since we won't be addressing all of your skin concerns right away.

However, if you're enrolled in my **Acne Program**, I highly recommend starting with your complete recommended routine, including your treatment products (actives). These play an important role in targeting acne, preventing new breakouts, and helping us achieve consistent results.

My goal is always to create a routine that's both effective and realistic for you, so we can discuss your priorities and budget together!

## "Which sunscreen should I use?"

Broad spectrum, SPF 30 or higher, every morning, enough of it (about 2 thin fingers worth), reapplied if you're outside or in a room with windows. Mineral if your skin is sensitive, acne-prone or freshly treated. The ones I carry are the ones I trust; ask me which fits you.

## "I'm running out before my next visit. What should I do?"

Don't stretch it or stop; message me and I'll set one aside for pickup, or order it from the shop with shipping. Running out of the treatment product for a week or two usually means starting back at a few nights a week.

## "How long does a bottle usually last?"

Roughly: a 1 oz serum, 2 to 3 months; a 2 oz moisturizer, 2 to 3 months; a 6 oz cleanser, 3 to 4 months; a 2 oz sunscreen used properly on the face, about a month. If something's lasting much longer, you may be using too little.

## "I skipped my routine for a few days. Do I start over?"

No, a few days doesn't undo anything. Pick it back up at the same strength. If it's been a week or more, go back to a few nights a week with your actives first, then build up again. Reach out if you're unsure.

## "I'm traveling. What should I bring?"

Cleanser, moisturizer, SPF and your main treatment product; leave the rest. Plane air is dry, so bring a hydrating mist or go heavier on moisturizer, and if you'll be in the sun, ease off the actives that week.
