# Questions clients ask: Results and how often

The exact questions clients text Evelyn, with her answers. Empty sections mean she hasn't answered yet: the assistant hands those questions to her.

## "How many facials until I see a difference?"

You'll see a glow after one, but real change takes a series: most people notice a difference by the third visit, with consistent home care in between. The home routine does most of the work; the facials keep it on track.

## "How often should I get a facial?"

Every 4 to 6 weeks for maintenance, which is about one skin cycle. If we're working on acne or a corrective series, every 2 to 4 weeks until we're where we want to be.

## "How long until my acne clears up?"

I tell everyone to give it 90 days to start seeing real results, and 3 to 6 months to be mostly clear. It depends on how severe it is, how consistent you are, and following the home care exactly. Some purging in the first few weeks is normal.

## "How often should I get waxed? How long does it last?"

Every 4 to 6 weeks for most areas; brows, lip and underarms often every 3 to 4. You'll be smooth for 2 to 3 weeks, and it lasts longer the more regular you are, because the hair grows back finer and sparser.

## "Does waxing hurt less over time?"

Yes, as long as you don't shave in between. The hair comes in finer and thinner each time, so every visit is quicker and less uncomfortable than the one before. The first is the hardest.

## "What can I do between visits to keep my results?"

Stick to the routine, SPF every day, hands off your face, clean pillowcases and phone screen, and don't start new products without checking with me. That's most of the result right there.
