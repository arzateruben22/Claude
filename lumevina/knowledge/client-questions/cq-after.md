# Questions clients ask: Right after a visit

The exact questions clients text Evelyn, with her answers. Empty sections mean she hasn't answered yet: the assistant hands those questions to her.

## "When can I wear makeup again?"

Give your skin the rest of the day with nothing on it. Mineral makeup is fine the next morning after a facial; after a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling, wait until it's no longer pink and tight, usually a full 24-48 hours.

## "Can I work out, swim, sauna or do hot yoga after?"

Skip heat and sweat for 24 hours after a facial or wax, and 48 hours after a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling: no gym, sauna, steam, hot yoga, hot tubs or swimming. Sweat and heat on freshly treated skin cause stinging and breakouts.

## "When can I wash my face, and with what?"

Leave it alone until tonight or tomorrow morning, then a gentle cleanser with lukewarm water, fingertips only. Moisturize and SPF in the morning. No scrubs, acids or exfoliating cleansers for a few days.

## "I broke out after my facial. Is that normal?"

If you are a part of my acne program and are on a new routine and starting acne treatments it is very normal to purge/breakout and the amount of time everyone purge varies. After normal custom facial a few small whiteheads a day or two after extractions is normal: what I loosened is surfacing. Don't pick, keep it simple and clean, and they settle within a few days. If it's spreading, painful or hot, send me a photo.

## "My skin is peeling after my peel. What do I do?"

Peeling usually starts on day 2 or 3 and finishes by day 5 to 7. Let it come off on its own: no picking, no scrubbing, no acids. Moisturize, SPF every morning, stay out of the sun. If anything blisters or weeps, message me.

## "When can I go back to my retinol or acids?"

After a facial, 2 or 3 days. After a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling, about a week, once the skin has stopped flaking and feels normal again. Then start back at a few nights a week, not every night.

## "I have little bumps after my wax. What should I do?"

Small red bumps for a few hours to a day are normal; it's the follicles reacting. Keep the area clean, cool and loose, no heat, sweat or tight clothes for a day or two, then exfoliate gently a few times a week to keep ingrowns away. If bumps turn into pus-filled spots or spread, message me right away.
