# Waxing (brows, lip, underarms and body): aftercare

## The first 24 hours

For 24 to 48 hours: no heat or sweat (workouts, hot showers, saunas, hot tubs, pools), no sun or tanning, no tight clothes on the area, no scented lotions, oils or deodorant. A soothing, fragrance-free product or aloe is fine.

## The first week

From day 2 or 3, exfoliate the area gently 2 or 3 times a week and keep it moisturized; that's what prevents ingrowns. No shaving between waxes. Book your next one for 4 to 6 weeks out.

## What's normal

Redness, warmth and small red bumps for a few hours up to a day, longer on a first wax or sensitive skin, and a few tiny white bumps a day or two later as the follicles settle.

## What isn't normal

Bumps that turn into pus-filled spots, spread or hurt, lifted or torn skin, blistering or a burn. Message me, and see a doctor for anything that looks infected.
