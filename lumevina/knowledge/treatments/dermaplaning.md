# Dermaplaning: aftercare

## The first 24 hours

No makeup for the rest of the day, no heat, sweat or sun, no touching. Tonight, a gentle cleanse and moisturizer; SPF in the morning. Your skin is extra smooth and extra bare, so it burns easily.

## The first week

Gentle cleanser, moisturizer and SPF daily; makeup glides on from the next day. No acids, retinol or scrubs for about 3 to 5 days, and no waxing or threading on the face for a week.

## What's normal

A little pink for a few hours and very smooth. The fine hair grows back exactly as it was, in 3 to 5 weeks; it does not come back thicker or darker.

## What isn't normal

Nicks that keep bleeding, a rash, hives, spreading redness or small pus-filled bumps. Message me with a photo.
