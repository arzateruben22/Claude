# Facials: aftercare

## The first 24 hours

Nothing on your skin for the rest of the day: no makeup, no touching or picking. No workouts, sauna, steam, hot yoga, hot showers on your face or swimming for 24 hours. Tonight or tomorrow morning, a gentle cleanse, moisturizer and SPF.

## The first week

Keep it simple: gentle cleanser, hydration, moisturizer, SPF every morning. No acids, retinol, scrubs or new products for 2 or 3 days. If it's a chemical peel or microchanneling treatment wait 7 days to introduce actives. Fresh pillowcase, clean phone screen, and let anything from extractions settle on its own.

## What's normal

A little pink or flushed for a few hours, slight tightness, mild tenderness where I did extractions, and maybe a few small whiteheads surfacing in the next day or two. All of that settles on its own.

## What isn't normal

Redness that spreads or gets worse after the first day, swelling (especially around the eyes or lips), hives, blistering, burning that doesn't settle, or a spot that's hot, painful or full of pus. Message me with a photo, and for anything spreading fast or any trouble breathing, see a doctor or call 911.
