# Brazilian, bikini and the mini vajacial: aftercare

## The first 24 hours

Loose cotton underwear, no tight clothes, no sex, no heat or sweat (gym, hot showers, baths, hot tubs, pools) and no sun on the area for 24 hours. Nothing scented, and hands off.

## The first week

Starting 2 or 3 days after, exfoliate gently a few times a week and keep the skin moisturized to prevent ingrowns. Don't dig at an ingrown; a warm compress and gentle exfoliation let it surface. No shaving in between, and book 4 to 6 weeks out.

## What's normal

Red, warm and a little tender for a few hours to a day, small red bumps, and a few tiny white bumps a day or two later. The first time is always the most reactive.

## What isn't normal

Pus-filled bumps that spread or hurt, lifted or torn skin, blistering, a rash with hives, or a painful ingrown that won't surface. Message me; anything infected should be seen by a doctor.
