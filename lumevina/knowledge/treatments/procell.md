# ProCell Microchanneling: aftercare

## The first 24 hours

90-minute downtime after treatment 
• Leave your skin untouched and free of products.
• Keep your hair up and away from your face.
• Avoid letting your phone, dog, hair, or anything else touch your face during this time.
• Wash your hands thoroughly before touching your face.

After 90 minutes You may apply your moisturizer and reapply as needed for dryness.

**Your same night routine 🌙**

Start by washing your hands thoroughly with soap and water, then:

1. Gently wash your face with your gentle cleanser.
2. Apply the mask (if it was given to you)
3. Apply Hydrating serum
4. Apply Moisturizer
5. Finish with a Balm if recommended to you

Use a fresh, clean towel to gently pat your skin dry—no rubbing. Continue using clean towels over the next few days, and **change to a clean pillowcase tonight.

## The first week

Gentle routine only (gentle cleanser - no active cleanser, hydrating toner, hydrating serum, moisturizer and MINERAL SPF only). No actives (acids, retinol, scrubs) for about a week, no sun or tanning, no waxing or other treatments on the area. Let any flaking fall on its own.

## What's normal

Red and warm for 12 to 48 hours, tightness, pinpoint marks, and light flaking around day 3. Collagen keeps building for weeks, so the real result shows between sessions, not the next day.

## What isn't normal

Swelling that grows, redness that spreads instead of fading, blistering, pus, heat or fever, or a cold sore starting. Message me right away; an infection or a cold sore needs a doctor quickly.
