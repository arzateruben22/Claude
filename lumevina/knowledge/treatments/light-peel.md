# Refresh Peel (no downtime): aftercare

## The first 24 hours

No makeup today, no heat, sweat, sun or swimming for 24 to 48 hours, no touching. Tonight or tomorrow, a gentle cleanse, moisturizer and SPF. Nothing exfoliating.

## The first week

Gentle cleanser, moisturizer, SPF every morning, hat if you're out. No acids, retinol, scrubs or waxing for about a week. If you flake, let it come off by itself.

## What's normal

Pink for an hour or two, a little tightness, and maybe light flaking on day 2 or 3. Some skin barely flakes at all and still gets the benefit.

## What isn't normal

Blistering, weeping, swelling, hives, spreading redness or burning that doesn't settle in a day. Send me a photo, and see a doctor if it's getting worse fast.
