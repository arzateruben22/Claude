## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

A quick sting per strip that fades right away; brows and lip are over in seconds. Underarms and the back are the most sensitive the first time.

## Who has to wait, or can't have it

No waxing on sunburned, broken or irritated skin, on an active cold sore (lip), over a prescription retinoid or acids used on that area that week, or during and for months after Accutane. Tell me first.

## Anything to do before this one, beyond your general list

A quarter inch of hair, no shaving in between, gentle exfoliation 2 or 3 days before, and clean, dry skin with no lotion or deodorant on the day.
