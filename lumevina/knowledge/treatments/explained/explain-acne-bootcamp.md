# Acne Bootcamp (consultation, monthly and bi-weekly): what to expect

## What happens, step by step

First visit: a consultation (your skin, routine, habits, what's driving it) and a treatment: double cleanse, exfoliation, extractions, calming and antibacterial steps, LED. You leave with a strict home routine we build up slowly. Then monthly treatments, or every two weeks for severe acne, with more time for peels and extractions.

## How often should they book it?

Every 2 weeks

Monthly for most; every two weeks for severe acne. Until we're clear, then maintenance monthly.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

Give it 90 days to see real results and 3 to 6 months to be mostly clear. Consistency at home and following the aftercare exactly make the difference; some purging in the first weeks is normal.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Anyone with breakouts, from congestion to inflamed acne, who's ready to commit to a home routine. Cystic, scarring or clearly hormonal acne may need a doctor alongside me, and I'll say so.
