# Brazilian, bikini and the mini vajacial: what to expect

## What happens, step by step

You undress from the waist down and lie on the table; I cleanse and prep the skin, apply warm hard wax in sections and remove it without strips, working front to back, then tweeze any strays and finish with a soothing product. The mini vajacial adds extractions, ingrown removal and a cooling mask.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

A quick sting as each section comes off, then it fades. Hard wax is gentler than strip wax. Breathe out as I pull, and it's over in 30 to 40 minutes. The first is the most sensitive.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

Red, warm and a little tender for a few hours; small bumps for up to a day. Loose clothes, no heat, sweat or sex for 24 hours, and you're fine.

## How often should they book it?

Every 4 to 6 weeks

Regular is easier: the hair grows back finer and each visit is quicker. No shaving in between.

## Who has to wait, or can't have it

Accutane in the last 6 to 12 months, a prescription retinoid on the area, a sunburn, broken or irritated skin, or an active infection: tell me first. Pregnancy waxing is common but more sensitive; check with your doctor if you're unsure.

## Anything to do before this one, beyond your general list

A quarter inch of hair (2 to 3 weeks after shaving), exfoliate 2 or 3 days before, shower that day, no lotion or oil on the area, loose cotton. An over-the-counter pain reliever 30 to 45 minutes before is your call.
