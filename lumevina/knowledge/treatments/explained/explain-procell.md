# ProCell Microchanneling: what to expect

## What happens, step by step

Cleanse, dermaplaning if added, peel if added, extractions, then the microchanneling itself: tiny, controlled channels across the skin that trigger collagen, while growth-factor serums go in. Then sheet masks, LED to calm, and you go home with the recovery set and post-care instructions. About an hour and ten minutes.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

With numbing, mostly a vibration and light scratching; the forehead and jaw are the most sensitive spots. Afterwards your skin feels warm and tight, like a sunburn. Numbing must be applied 30 min before appointment and I will rinse it off for you. Patch test is recommended on your wrist first before appointment.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

Red like a sunburn for 12 to 48 hours, tightness, sometimes pinpoint marks or light flaking by day 3. No makeup for 24 hours, no sweat or sun for 48. Plan it at least a week before anything important.

## How often should they book it?

A series, then maintenance

4 weeks apart. A minimum of 4 treatments; up to 8 depending on your concerns. Then a session every few months to maintain.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

Collagen builds over weeks, so you see the change between sessions: smoother texture, softer scars and lines, smaller-looking pores, more even tone, with the biggest difference after the full series. Results last with maintenance and daily SPF.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Acne scars, uneven texture, enlarged pores, fine lines, pigmentation and overall skin quality. Not for severe active, inflamed acne (we clear that first), open skin, or active cold sores.

## Who has to wait, or can't have it

If you're on Accutane or finished it in the last 6 to 12 months, have active breakouts or cold sores, are pregnant, scar with keloids, or take blood thinners, tell me first: some of those wait, some need your doctor's OK. Pause retinoids and acids for a week before.

## Anything to do before this one, beyond your general list

No retinoids, acids, scrubs or dermaplaning for a week before, no sun or tanning, no waxing on the face that week, and come with clean skin and no makeup.
