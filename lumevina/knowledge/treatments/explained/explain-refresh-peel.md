# Refresh Peel: what to expect

## What happens, step by step

Double cleanse, prep, then the peel goes on for a few minutes while I watch your skin, then it's neutralized or removed, followed by extractions I finish with calming products LED therapy and SPF. It's a full treatment.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

Warm and tingly for a few minutes, sometimes a little itchy, then it fades. I'm fanning you the whole time.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

Pretty much none: pink for an hour or two, maybe light flaking on day 2 or 3. Makeup the next day. A lunchtime peel.

## How often should they book it?

Every 3 to 4 weeks

A series of 3 to 6 is where it makes a visible difference; then as a boost with your facials.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

Brighter, smoother skin within days, and with a series, better texture, tone and fewer blemishes. Kept with your routine and SPF.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Dullness, mild texture, congestion, early dark marks, or a glow before an event with no time to peel. Sensitive or very reactive skin, and anything open or inflamed, waits.

## Who has to wait, or can't have it

Accutane in the last 6 to 12 months, a prescription retinoid in the last week, a sunburn or tan, active cold sores, a recent laser or peel, pregnancy, or a named skin condition: tell me first, and some of those need your doctor's OK.

## Anything to do before this one, beyond your general list

Pause retinoids and acids for 3 to 5 days (a prescription retinoid, about a week), no waxing or dermaplaning on the face that week, no sun, and come with clean skin.
