# Ageless Grace Facial: what to expect

## What happens, step by step

Double cleanse, exfoliation, extractions if needed, then the age-supporting part: oxygen therapy and a professional Tightening Protein Booster to lift, firm and brighten, with massage, LED and cryo globes. Add dermaplaning or a peel for more exfoliation and glow; I'll tell you whether one or both fit your skin.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

Cool, plumping and relaxing. The oxygen feels like a cool breeze; the booster is a warm, tightening mask. Nothing uncomfortable.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

None. You leave glowing and can go straight to dinner; makeup the next morning. With a peel added, light flaking on day 2 or 3 is possible.

## How often should they book it?

Every 4 to 6 weeks

Monthly is where it shines; before an event, book it 3 to 5 days ahead.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

Firmer, smoother, brighter skin the same day, lasting a week or two, and it builds with regular visits. For lasting firmness it pairs with a home routine (retinoid, peptides, SPF) and, for deeper change, ProCell.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Fine lines, loss of firmness, dullness, dehydration, or anyone who wants a glow before an event. Suitable for all skin types. Active breakouts are better served by the acne treatments first.
