# Dermaplaning (as an add-on): what to expect

## What happens, step by step

Clean, dry skin, then a sterile blade glides over the face at an angle, lifting dead surface cells and the fine vellus hair. A few minutes per area, then calming products and SPF, or straight into your facial or peel.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

Painless; a light scraping sensation, like a soft brush. Most people find it relaxing.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

None. A little pink for a few hours, smooth right away, makeup glides on from the next day. SPF, hydration and proper moisturizer matters more than usual for a few days.

## How often should they book it?

Every 4 to 6 weeks

As the fine hair returns, usually 3 to 5 weeks.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

Instantly smoother, brighter skin and makeup that sits better; products and peels absorb better afterwards. The hair grows back exactly as it was, not thicker or darker.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Dullness, peach fuzz, dry flaky skin, makeup that cakes, or as an add-on to a facial or peel. Not for severe active breakouts, open skin, very reactive skin or active cold sores.

## Who has to wait, or can't have it

Active or pustular breakouts, Accutane in the last 6 to 12 months, a prescription retinoid that week, a sunburn, cold sores, or a recent peel or laser on the face: tell me first.
