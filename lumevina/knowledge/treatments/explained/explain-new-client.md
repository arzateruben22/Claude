# New Clients Start Here: what to expect

## What happens, step by step

We talk first: your concerns, your current routine, your goals. Then a fully customized facial: double cleanse, enzyme exfoliation, extractions, a targeted mask, massage, LED and cryo globes. We end with your home routine and the plan for next time.

## Does it hurt? How does it feel?

Relaxing, mostly. Extractions can pinch a little for a minute, and the exfoliation can tingle; nothing that hurts. Tell me if anything stings and I adjust.

## How they'll look after, and for how long

None. A little pink for an hour or two, maybe a small bump or two surfacing in the next day where I did extractions. You can go about your day; just no makeup, heat or sweat until tomorrow.

## How often should they book it?

Every 4 to 6 weeks

After your first visit you book the Lumevina Custom Facial as a returning client; every 2 to 4 weeks if we're working on something specific.

## When will they see results, and how long do they last?

A glow right away. Real change shows over a few visits with the home routine in between: usually a noticeable difference by the third visit.

## Who it's best for, and who should book something else

Anyone new to me without acne concerns: dullness, dryness, texture, fine lines, maintenance. New clients with acne concerns book the Acne Bootcamp consultation + treatment instead.
