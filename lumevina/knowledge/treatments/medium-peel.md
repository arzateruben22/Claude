# Corrective Peel (medium depth): aftercare

## The first 24 hours

Leave your skin completely alone: no makeup, no touching, no heat, sweat, sun or swimming for 48 hours. Only the products I recommended. Sleep on a clean pillowcase and keep your face out of hot water.

## The first week

Peeling usually starts on day 2 or 3 and finishes by day 5 to 7. Don't pick, pull or exfoliate it off; it will leave marks. Moisturize often, SPF every morning, stay out of the sun, and no actives, waxing or other treatments for a week to ten days.

## What's normal

Pink to red for a day or two, tightness, and darker or blotchy-looking skin before it flakes; that's the old surface lifting, not the result. Visible peeling on days 2 to 5.

## What isn't normal

Blistering, weeping or crusting, swelling, hives, redness that spreads or gets worse after day two, severe pain, or a cold sore starting. Message me with a photo right away; some of those need a doctor the same day.
