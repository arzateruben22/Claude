# Visits and the studio

## What should they wear or bring?

Just yourself. Come with a clean face if you can (I'll remove makeup if not), apply moisturizer and SPF day before appointment, hair you don't mind getting a little damp at the hairline, and for a wax, loose comfortable clothing. Bring a list or photos of what you're using at home.

## Can they arrive wearing makeup?

Yes, I'll remove it

Bare-faced saves a little time, but don't worry about it; a double cleanse is the first step of every facial.

## Can they bring a friend, partner or child?

It's a small, one-on-one studio, so the treatment room is just you and me. A friend can wait for you, and if you're under 18, a parent comes with you. Little ones can't be in the room during a treatment.

## What happens at a first visit, from the moment they arrive?

We start by talking: your skin, what you're using now, what you want. I look at your skin up close, then we go into a fully customized facial: double cleanse, enzyme exfoliation, extractions, a targeted mask, massage, LED and cryo globes. You leave with a home routine and a plan for the next visit.
