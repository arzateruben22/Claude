# Aftercare, in general

General esthetics knowledge, not Evelyn's words. The usual aftercare for each kind of treatment. Evelyn's own aftercare, when she has written it, is the one to give; this fills gaps and explains why.

## After any facial, peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling

**The first 24 hours**
- Leave the skin alone: no makeup the rest of the day (mineral makeup is often allowed the next morning), no touching or picking.
- No heat or sweat: skip workouts, saunas, steam rooms, hot yoga, hot showers on the face and swimming (chlorine and salt) for 24 to 48 hours. Sweat and heat on freshly exfoliated skin cause stinging and breakouts.
- Cleanse gently that night or the next morning with a mild cleanser and lukewarm water; moisturize; SPF in the morning.
- No actives: acids, retinoids, scrubs, benzoyl peroxide and exfoliating toners stay off for at least two or three days after a facial, five to seven days after a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling, or until the skin has stopped flaking and feels normal.
- Sun: avoid direct sun and tanning for a week, SPF every morning, reapplied outdoors.

**The first week**
- Hydrate and protect: gentle cleanser, hydrating serum or mist, moisturizer, SPF.
- Let flaking fall on its own. Peeling skin that's pulled off leaves dark marks or raw patches.
- Change pillowcases and clean phone screens, especially after extractions.
- Hold off on waxing, threading, dermaplaning at home, facial scrubs and new products for a week.

**What's normal**
- Pink or slightly flushed skin for a few hours (longer after a peel).
- Tightness and dryness for a day or two.
- Light flaking on days two to five after a peel, dermaplaning or microchanneling.
- A few small whiteheads or bumps in the day or two after extractions, as what was loosened surfaces; a short "purge" in acne-prone skin after a peel.
- Mild tenderness where extractions were done.

**What isn't normal, and means message Evelyn or see a doctor**
- Redness that spreads or gets worse after the first day instead of better.
- Swelling, especially around the eyes or lips; hives; blistering; weeping or crusting.
- Burning that doesn't settle in a day, or pain.
- Pus, heat and spreading redness around a spot (possible infection), or fever.
- A cold sore starting (lip tingling or blisters): a doctor can prescribe something fast.
For breathing trouble or swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, call 911.

## After a peel in particular

Peeling, if it comes, usually starts on day two or three and finishes by day five to seven. Don't pick or exfoliate it off. Keep the skin moisturized and out of the sun; a hat helps. Skin can look darker or blotchy before it flakes; that's the old surface lifting, not the result.

## After dermaplaning

Skin is smooth and a little pink for a few hours. Makeup glides on the next day. No exfoliants for a few days; SPF daily, since the new surface burns easily. Hair returns as it was, in three to five weeks.

## After microchanneling

Red like a sunburn for 12 to 48 hours, warm and tight, sometimes pinpoint marks or light flaking by day three. Only the products given at the visit for the first day or two; no makeup for 24 hours; no sweat, heat or sun for 48 hours; no actives for about a week. Collagen builds for weeks, so the real result shows between sessions.

## Why the rules exist

Exfoliated, extracted or waxed skin has a thinner barrier and open follicles for a day or two. Heat, sweat, friction, bacteria (hands, phones, pillowcases, gym equipment) and strong actives in that window are what turn a good treatment into redness, bumps or dark marks.
