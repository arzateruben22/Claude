Simulated crowd

Stoppage Time is a fictional sports bar. The crowd, the matches on the screens and the people here are simulated from the time of day and the day of the week. Nothing here is live, and nobody here is a real person or another player.

The name, the hours, the menu, the screens and the sign are ours. The streets are the real ones, as OpenStreetMap maps them.
