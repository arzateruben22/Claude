Recognition beats spectacle. Everyone in OC has an exit, and the game starts at it. A clip that shows a viewer's exit sign gets a comment, and a comment asking for their exit is the cheapest engagement there is.

The honesty lines

  • The roads, ramps and exit signs are real, from OpenStreetMap via Overture Maps.
  • Traffic and weather are simulated. No live traffic, no live weather, no multiplayer.
  • Scores stay on your phone. There's no online leaderboard.
  • The cars are our own makes, Rossini and Vela. Never call one anything real.
  • A game. Drive the real ones at the limit.

The last line goes on every clip and every post.

Never say

  • Real traffic
  • Live weather
  • Race your friends
  • Online leaderboard
  • Lamborghini, Ferrari, Porsche
  • Disney, or any business, in a tag
  • Lot 57 is a real bar

The wordmark is the face of every account, the end card and the waitlist page, always next to the honesty line, never next to a speed number on its own. Leave "Top Speed" out of handles, tags, titles, the profile picture, the end card and the press kit until the trademark search clears.

Guide4

Shoot and post a clip

The game's own tools, a phone and $0. This is clip 8.1, the hook; every other clip changes only the settings.

  1. Step1

    Start at your exit

    Start panel → Start at your exit → I-405 North → Jamboree Road. Free Drive. You start 450 m before the sign.

  2. Step2

    Set the scene

    Time Sunset, traffic Moderate, camera Driver's seat. Graphics on the preset the phone holds at 30 fps or better: Low until Gate 0 says otherwise.

  3. Step3

    Corner window off

    Set the corner window to Off for a clean frame. The driving HUD is already minimal.

  4. Step4

    Screen record, portrait, sound on

    Control Center on iPhone, Quick Settings on Android. The radio's music is composed in code, so there's nothing for Content ID to match.

    Before Gate 0 Record on a laptop and label it "dev build · laptop", or post stills. Never caption a desktop run as the phone.

  5. Step5

    Drive it: 18 seconds

    0–2 s the Jamboree Road sign. 2–10 s floor it, two clean passes. 10–14 s switch to Chase as the airport sign goes by. 14–16.5 s back to the seat, the 55 split ahead.

  6. Step6

    Trim, text, end card

    Trim in the phone's own editor to 12–25 s. Text in the middle 70% of the frame: "The real 405. At sunset." then "Every exit is real." Add the 1.5-second wordmark end card.

  7. Step7

    Caption and five tags

    One or two plain sentences, the honesty line, five tags at most: always #OCUltra #OrangeCounty, plus three.

    Caption, clip 8.1
    I'm building Orange County's real freeways for your phone. This is the 405 North at Jamboree. A game. Drive the real ones at the limit.
#OCUltra #OrangeCounty #405 #Irvine #gamedev
  8. Step8

    Post the original, three times

    Upload the phone's original recording to TikTok, Reels and Shorts, never a download from another app (watermarked reposts reach fewer people). On TikTok: Content disclosure and ads → Disclose commercial content → Your brand.

  9. Step9

    Reply, then count

    Answer comments for the day. Write the views and the sign-ups by the post's code into the weekly report.

    Done when The clip is live on three apps and its sign-ups are counted.

The scarcity is real. Keep it that way.

The real limits are OC-only until Day 46, 25 then 50 then 100 testers, and the Founder Pack's "sold until 31 December 2026, then never again". The OC Hypercar Founder Edition, once it is in the game, is "sold until 31 December 2026" and no more: whether it is ever sold again is still the owner's decision, so never "never again" for the car, and its numbers have no cap, so never "only N left". Say exact numbers. Never "only 3 spots left" unless the database counted it that minute. No countdowns that reset: the FTC's 2022 dark-patterns report names false scarcity and baseless timers as deceptive.

Referral rewards that stay clean

Places on the list and one free, earned-only "Beta '26" badge (never sold, separate from the paid Founder badge). No money, no prizes, and never the Founder Pack as a prize. A credit is provisional until the friend reaches the menu. At most 10 credits per person; twenty sign-ups from one link in a minute is a bot. No referral links where a community bans them.

Guide7

The Founders crew and the FTC rule

The business draft's paid ambassadors ($200–500 a month) become an unpaid crew at $0. Being in the crew is a perk, and under the FTC's Endorsement Guides a perk is a material connection, so every crew post about the game discloses, every time.

  1. Step1

    Recruit 5 to 10

    Week of 9 Nov. Volunteers who live in OC, are 18 or older, already play the beta and post anyway. Nobody under 18 in a promotional role.

  2. Step2

    Say what they get

    Their name on the Founders credits, builds a few days early, their own CREW-[name] code and a #founders channel. Optionally a free Founder Pack once the store is live, granted by 31 Dec 2026. No money.

  3. Step3

    Ask for little

    Whatever they'd post anyway, two posts a week at most, and one short weekly note: posts, what players asked for, what broke and on which phone.

  4. Step4

    Disclose in the video itself

    On-screen text in the first seconds, readable and not in a corner; said out loud if they talk. A disclosure only in the description or comments isn't enough.

  5. Step5

    And at the start of the caption

    "Ad:", "#ad" or a plain sentence first, not lost among hashtags. Plus the platform's label: TikTok's Branded content, Instagram's paid partnership, YouTube's paid promotion (being renamed "branded content"). The FTC says use the platform's tool as well as your own, not instead.

  6. Step6

    Never reviews, never ratings

    Never ask anyone for a rating or review, and never reward one. The FTC's Consumer Review Rule (in force 21 Oct 2024) bans paying for reviews of a given slant and buying fake followers or views; penalties run up to $53,088 per violation (the 2025 level, kept for 2026; verify in January 2027).

  7. Step7

    Check every post

    Look at each crew post that week for the disclosure, the honesty line, and no real-world driving.

  8. Step8

    The strike rule

    The first miss gets a polite edit request. A second miss ends the perks.

    Done when Every perk-holder's post says "#ad" or "got this free".

Counts as a disclosure

  • "Ad"
  • "#ad"
  • "I'm on the OC Ultra Founders crew (early builds)"
  • "Got this free from OC Ultra"
  • "#OCUltraAmbassador" may be enough per the FTC's FAQ; "#ad" or a plain sentence is safer

Doesn't count

  • "#ambassador" alone: ambiguous unless joined to the brand's name
  • "sp", "spon", "collab" or a lone "thanks"
  • A disclosure only in the bio, the profile or the comments

Creators outside the crew: after Day 46, a creator who gets nothing but a free game anyone can play has no material connection. Before Day 46 the code itself is early access, which the 2023 Endorsement Guides list as a material connection (16 CFR 255.5), so a light "got an early beta code from the dev" keeps it clean.

Exit10

Community norms

For the owner, the crew, and anyone who posts for OC Ultra.

  1. One person, one account. No second accounts, no friends posting "I just found this" without saying they know you, no paid comments.
  2. Never ask for votes, stars, ratings or reviews, anywhere, and never reward one. Ask people to play and to tell us what broke.
  3. Buy nothing: followers, views, upvotes, ratings or "launch services".
  4. Disclose every perk. When unsure, disclose.
  5. No fake scarcity. Real numbers only.
  6. Only true claims. Real roads, simulated traffic and weather, no multiplayer, our own car makes, Lot 57 is fictional. Numbers on screen come from real runs.
  7. No real-world driving footage, ever, and nothing that reads as a dare or goes near a sideshow.
  8. Read the room. Contribute before you promote, post once per community, never cross-post the same text the same day.
  9. No unsolicited DMs, in bulk, to anyone. One message, one follow-up at most.
  10. Minors: never DM a student; schools only through teachers or advisors; nobody under 13 targeted; the crew is 18+.
  11. Criticism gets thanks and a fix. If a post was wrong, correct it in a reply; don't quietly delete it.
  12. Answer within 48 hours, even if the answer is "logged, not fixed yet".
