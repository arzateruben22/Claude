OC Ultra · the 30 to 90 day plan

Ninety days. Four phases. $0 up front.

The road from today's test build to a public Orange County launch and a decision on Day 90. Every step names who does it, what it costs, the proof that it worked and what to do if it didn't. Gates move the calendar; dates don't.

  • Mon 19 OctDay 1, if the owner says go
  • Thu 3 DecDay 46, the public OC launch
  • Thu 31 DecDay 74, Founder Pack sales end (and the OC Hypercar Founder Edition's, if it is on sale by then)
  • Sat 16 JanDay 90, the decision gates
Exit1

The honest shape

A hobby-scale launch of a free browser game, played on phones and installed from the browser with Add to Home Screen.

Costs

$0

is required. Every paid item is optional and has a trigger. Buying only the domain makes the 90 days cost about $11.

Earns

$0–850

from the store over 90 days, most likely under $100. That is less than the optional costs if the owner bought them all.

The point

Players

People in OC driving their own exit, coming back, and telling us what broke. Say so to anyone who asks about money.

The money rule

Payments are built and stay in test mode until the owner connects Stripe and Supabase and turns live mode on. Cosmetics, expansions and support only; never pay-to-win (the owner's one exception, the OC Hypercar Founder Edition, is faster in Free Drive only and at or under the best earnable car in every other mode; not yet in CLAUDE.md), never the earned credits, no loot boxes, no premium currency, no ads at launch. Everything in the game today stays free. Purchases are 13+, and nothing is sold before it's in the game.

Owner time (an estimate)

About 8 hours a week in weeks 1–2, about 6 during the beta, about 15 in launch week, and 4 to 6 after that. Protect it: a solo builder who burns out on Day 50 loses the whole plan. Claude builds; the owner holds accounts and keys, posts, meets players and decides.

Gate 0, before any marketing that sends people to the game

The game must reach the menu at a playable frame rate on three phones: the owner's, one other iPhone and one Android (borrowed, $0). Each phone does three runs on Wi-Fi, and every run must:

Exit3

How to pump it out

Every build, beta or public, follows the same eight steps. Claude runs it; the owner says go at step 5. From Day 60 the weekly build becomes hotfix-only and a named build ships the first Thursday of each month.

  1. Step1

    Run the suite

    npm run build && npm test on the change (10 to 13 minutes here), plus npm run store:check.

    Done when Every group is green.

  2. Step2

    Phones, if it touched loading

    Re-run Gate 0's checks on two phones at least if the change touched rendering, loading or memory.

    Done when Menu reached at 30 fps or better on Low.

  3. Step3

    Version and patch notes

    Bump the version (the Feedback and boot reports print it) and write the patch notes in plain words.

  4. Step4

    Portal builds

    Build the itch.io and portal variants, if any portals are live.

  5. Step5

    Owner: go

    Nothing ships without the owner's word.

  6. Step6

    Deploy and smoke-test

    Deploy to the org's Pages repo, then on a phone over cellular: menu reached, a 60-second drive, and a test-mode purchase on a staging copy if the store is live.

  7. Step7

    Announce

    Patch notes in #announcements and on the site.

  8. Step8

    Broken? Roll back

    Revert the deploy commit (the drill's under-5-minute path), then fix.

    Bandwidth GitHub Pages' 100 GB a month soft limit is about 28,500 first loads of the 3.5 MB build; the service worker makes repeat visits nearly free.

Exit6

The decision gates

Days 88 to 90. The thresholds are this plan's own, set from the metric targets, not industry rules. The owner can move them before Day 60, never on the day.

A

Keep the web build as the product

Go if
Load success at least 90% on phones, day 7 return at least 6%, and at least 300 players reached the menu in the last 30 days.
Then
Keep the monthly rhythm. Next 90 days: LA County (re-check the data budget first: OC's road file alone is 2.3 MB), more exits and races.
Otherwise
Load success under 90%: a performance-only month. Day 7 under 3% with good loading: the hook or the loop is wrong; build the three most-asked features, test one new hook, or pause and say so.
B1

Buy the Google Play account ($25 once)

Go if
At least 150 Android players a week, Android load success at least 90%, and 12 testers willing to stay opted in for 14 days.
Then
Register, wrap the PWA as a Trusted Web Activity (Bubblewrap or PWABuilder, free), and run the required closed test: about 3 weeks to a listing. From 1 Jan 2027 a store app must also read California's age signal (AB 1043).
Otherwise
Stay on the PWA and re-check monthly.
B2

Buy the Apple Developer account ($99 a year)

Go if
At least 200 iOS players a week, at least 25 asking for an App Store version, iOS load success at least 90% on two iPhone generations, and a plan for Guideline 4.2.
Then
A native wrapper with real native features (haptics, offline, share, Game Center), not a website in a frame. Check the purchase rules first: Apple's commission on US link-out sales is still in court. Verify at launch.
Otherwise
Stay on the PWA. Home-screen web apps already install, work offline and can get push.
C

Start the UE5 track

Go if
Phase 0 only (the spec pack and engine-free core, $0 in cloud sessions) if the web game holds at least 1,000 monthly players and the owner wants the desktop game. Phase 1 only with a UE-capable PC or a cloud GPU, after two months of the monthly rhythm.
Then
Phase 0: 2–3 weeks. Phase 1: 4–8 weeks for one engineer (the migration doc's estimates).
Otherwise
No. The web game is the product; revisit at Day 180.

What could go wrong, and what we do

  • High until Gate 0The game runs badly on phones

    Gate 0 before any marketing; the boot report and safe mode; Low as the phone default; waves held below 90% load success.

  • MediumA crash or a bad build on launch day

    Build freeze from Day 44, the rollback drill, a store off-switch, a second host ready.

  • MediumPlatform rules change

    Nothing depends on an app store in these 90 days. Re-read each channel's rules the day you post.

  • MediumA name clash

    The trademark search before anything is printed; OC Ultra alone in public; backup names ready.

  • High if one shipsHarassment through a public leaderboard
