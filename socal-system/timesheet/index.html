Rules

What the engine applies, in plain words. Authority: the SoCal dispatch rulebook (last updated 2026-07-31).

This tool computes company rulebook rules. It is not legal advice and it is not payroll. Where the rulebook has no answer, the result carries a Pending QC ruling flag instead of a guess, and the hours are coded the conservative way described below.

The window: PW vs PWSS

  • The window is 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM by the clock.
  • Every paid minute (work, travel, paid break) is counted by its actual clock time. Majority inside the window: PW. Majority outside: PWSS (special shift).
  • Exact 50/50: the shift codes to the side where it started.
  • One family per shift. It prefixes every labor code: PW Regular, PW 1.5 OT, PW 2.0 DT, PWSS Regular, PWSS 1.5 OT, PWSS 2.0 DT.
  • Minutes after midnight count by their real clock time on day two. Lunch is unpaid and never counts.

The ladder: Regular, 1.5 OT, 2.0 DT

  • Minutes 1 to 480 (the first 8 hours) are Regular. Minutes 481 to 720 (hours 8 to 12) are 1.5 OT. Minute 721 onward (past 12 hours, i.e. after 4 hours of OT) is 2.0 DT.
  • The count belongs to the calendar day, not the shift: at midnight it restarts at zero for the Shift Change row, because the post-midnight hours are the next day's hours (rulebook: "any hours past 8 in a day").
  • The ladder starts where the day ledger says, not at zero. See below.

Saturday and Sunday

  • Saturday: every minute is at least 1.5 OT. Sunday: every minute is 2.0 DT.
  • Applied per calendar day of each minute. A Friday shift that spills past midnight has its Saturday minutes at 1.5; Saturday into Sunday goes to 2.0 after midnight; Sunday into Monday drops back to the plain ladder.
  • Holidays have no rule on file. A holiday shift is coded like a weekday and flagged for QC.

Lunch

  • The first lunch must begin no later than 4:59 after the shift starts (elapsed clock time). A shift under 5 hours with no lunch is fine.
  • Over 12 hours worked: a second lunch must begin no later than 9:59. Between 10 and 12 hours the second lunch is waivable; the tool notes it either way.
  • A missing or late lunch is an error and blocks Submit.
  • Rest breaks are advisory only: a paid stretch over 4 hours with no break or lunch gets a warning (California 10-minute paid rest per 4 hours), pending a company policy ruling.

Midnight: the Shift Change row

  • A shift that crosses midnight gets a second checklist row dated the next day at 12:00 AM with Status Shift Change. The results card shows both rows.
  • Hours after midnight are coded by their own calendar day (so a Friday night shift's Saturday minutes are OT) and the 8-hour clock restarts at midnight, the same way the day ledger below counts them.

Day ledger: hours already worked today

  • Overtime belongs to the person's workday (midnight to midnight), not to a job. A second shift or a second job on the same date does not restart the 8-hour clock.
  • The Entry screen prefills "Hours already worked today" from every other entry on file for that inspector on that date, including the post-midnight spill of a shift that started the day before.
  • Override it, with a note, when hours exist outside this system (another employer, or a day that was never entered). The override is recorded with the entry.

Timeline checks

  • Blocks sort by start time. A block that starts earlier than the previous block's stop is placed on day two.
  • Overlapping blocks and zero-length blocks are errors. A gap between blocks is a warning (off the clock, not paid). More than 20 hours elapsed is an error (almost certainly a typo).

Miles and EA

  • Miles come from the daily log odometer: ending minus beginning. An ending reading below the beginning is an error. Typed miles that do not match the odometer are a warning.
  • EA format is a warning only: 105 Express EAs end in 4 (07-314524). D8 EAs appear two ways: the Daily Report form uses 08-1C08U3 (form note: all EA #'s end in 3) and the checklist uses 1C08U4; both are accepted, the 4-form gets an info note. CTC has no EA rule.
  • Non-PW hours (interviews, PTO) are not an entry type in this version.

Open rulings: pending QC

Six questions the rulebook leaves open. The engine never guesses on them; it flags the entry and codes it as described.

  1. Pending QC rulingSaturday after 4 hours of OT. Saturday is 1.5 all day. Does the "after 4 hours of 1.5, the rest is 2.0" rule roll Saturday hours 5+ to 2.0? Current behavior: hours stay at 1.5 unless the ladder itself passes 12 hours of work; any Saturday shift over 4 paid hours is flagged with both readings.
  2. Pending QC rulingHoliday coding and pay. No holiday rule exists. The Holiday toggle codes the day like a normal weekday and flags it. Premium pay is never assumed.
  3. Pending QC rulingWhittier Lab break lines. Observed: lab break lines are always PW Regular, even at 10:15 PM on Saturday 7/4. Are lab lines exempt from the shift rules? This tool applies the shift rules to every block; QC decides whether lab lines get an exemption.
  4. Pending QC rulingNight shifts across midnight. The rulebook puts overtime on "hours past 8 in a day" and carries post-midnight hours on a next-day Shift Change row. Real invoices confirm the day is the unit across separate shifts (a second shift after an 8-hour morning was billed entirely as OT). Whether one continuous night shift restarts its 8-hour clock at midnight is undecided. Current behavior: the clock restarts at 00:00; any overnight shift where the continuous reading would differ is flagged with both sets of hours.
  5. Pending QC rulingWhich D8 EA goes on the timesheet. Daily Report form: 08-1C08U3. Checklist: 1C08U4. The app accepts both and notes the difference.
  6. Pending QC rulingRest-break policy. California provides a paid 10-minute rest per 4 hours. Company policy on tracking and enforcing it is not written down, so the engine only warns.
