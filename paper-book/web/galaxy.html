        How to read it

        • Star size: how often it's been traded. Bright core: it made money. Red: it lost.
        • Planets: each closed trade, the newest ten per star. Mint won, coral lost.
        • A pulsing ring: a position that's open right now.
        • New coins and stocks join on the outer arms, so each galaxy grows outward over time.
