## Shot prompts (paste one at a time)

Shot 1 (the hook): A soft, close cross-section of forehead skin between the brows, showing the smooth skin surface on top,
and beneath it the thin, fan-shaped muscle that pulls the brows together, its fibres gently bunched as if starting to
contract, with fine pale-gold nerve branches reaching the muscle. No face, only the tissue layers.

Shot 2 (the nerve arrives): A single pale-gold nerve fibre travelling through soft rose connective tissue and ending in a
rounded, translucent nerve ending that rests on the surface of a striped muscle fibre below. Calm, wide framing.

Shot 3 (the messenger is ready): Close-up inside the translucent nerve ending: many small round sacs (vesicles) filled with
tiny glowing particles, gathered near the bottom edge that faces the muscle. Soft light, the muscle fibre just visible
below.

Shot 4 (the message is sent): The vesicles at the bottom of the nerve ending opening and releasing a gentle cloud of tiny
teal molecules into the narrow gap above the muscle fibre. The molecules drift down toward the folded muscle surface.

Shot 5 (the muscle answers): The striped muscle fibre receiving the teal molecules on its folded surface; the fibre's
stripes drawn slightly closer together, as if contracting. Peach and rose tones, soft depth.

Shot 6 (something gets inside): The same nerve ending, now with a few small, smooth, slightly darker translucent particles
(a different shape from the teal messengers) entering the nerve ending through its outer membrane. Calm, not threatening.

Shot 7 (the message is held back): Inside the nerve ending: the vesicles are full but stay inside, unable to reach the
membrane; a delicate strand-like protein near the membrane is shown cut into two loose ends. No teal molecules in the gap
below. Quiet and still.

Shot 8 (relaxed and smooth): The forehead tissue cross-section again, now with the muscle fibres long and relaxed and the
skin surface above smooth and even. In the background, fine new pale-gold nerve sprouts beginning to grow toward the
muscle. Hopeful, soft morning light.
