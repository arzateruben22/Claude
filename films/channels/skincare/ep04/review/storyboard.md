# No. 004, "The deadliest dose": storyboard, look 2 (one line per sentence)

The look-test-2 rebuild (2 Nov). It has four hero shots. Each has one subject filling the frame and one slow move. The atmosphere is studio light, a shaft with dust, haze cards between depth layers, and shallow depth of field. The UV scan (the signature switch) lands on the key fact: the dose. Every cut lands on a spoken word (`compile.py` SHOTS).

**Hero shots**
- **A. The brow:** the frown as a skin macro in studio light. It is `set/brow04.js` restyled with `skinMaterial`, light tone, its relief deepened. A raking warm key comes from the left.
- **B. The junction:** the neuromuscular junction at cell scale (`set/nmj04.js`). A translucent nerve ending (`cellMaterial`) holds glowing cyan vesicles (`nucleusMaterial`) and rests on a banded muscle fibre (`strandMaterial`). Violet haze sits behind it, with dust in a shaft.
- **C. The cut face:** the stained micrograph block "below the skin" (`set/ref` SECTION, public domain).
- **D. The dose:** brow A again. The cyan scan line sweeps down on "twenty", and the brow turns to the UV instrument's lilac with one faint cyan glow (an illustration). The dose plate sets 20 units against ≈ 3,000.
