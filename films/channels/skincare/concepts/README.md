# Outer Layer reboot: three look concepts (owner's pick)

Each is a real kit render at 1080×1920 (`kits/cinema/film/render.mjs --tier final`), all on the same moment:
"sunscreen vs no sunscreen, 10 years". Every comparison is labelled a model, not a photo. Sources: `A/`, `B/`, `C/`
(film.json, scene.mjs, theme.json); fonts from `../fonts/` plus Bricolage Grotesque (OFL, `fonts/`) for C.

**A.jpg: UV camera.** Skin through a 365 nm camera on an indigo night ground: sunscreen reads dark, hidden sun damage
shows as freckles; a viewfinder frame, violet and cyan, and a daylight strip of three tones for the human view.

**B.jpg: Macro cinema.** A low drone over skin at golden hour: the microrelief as terrain, pores as craters, hairs as
trees, warm rim light, soft distance; the protected half satin-smooth, the unprotected half deeper-lined and spotted.

**C.jpg: Bold editorial.** A beauty-magazine spread: cobalt and acid-lime blocks, giant Bricolage Grotesque type, hard
tags; the 3D moment is two cut sections of the same skin side by side, and a three-tone strip.

None uses Lumevina's black, rose or gold. Captions and labels sit inside the 9:16 safe box on plates at ≥ 4.5:1.
