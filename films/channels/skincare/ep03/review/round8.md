## Blocker: (h), the section blocks, 5.6–39, 39.3–51.5 and 61.6–66

- **Collagen (crop at 10.0):** at 540 px the bundles still read as smooth satin tubes. The fibre micro-surface is not visible at this size.
  - **Fix:** raise the fibre relief, or the scale of its detail, until a 2× crop of a 540 frame shows lengthwise striation. If the fix only shows at final resolution, attach one 1080 still of the weave with the next draft, so (h) is judged at delivery size.
- **The patch (19–39):** identical, evenly spaced white bars with flat ends are the most obviously computer-generated object in the film, and they sit on screen for 20 s.
  - **Fix:** irregular strands that vary in thickness and spacing, with the same fibre surface but paler and denser. This was not done in r8.
- **Rim and void:** the rim on the block edges is still hard to see against the haze. The slab still ends on a flat dark floor at the bottom of most section frames.
  - **Fix:** a warmer, brighter rim on the edge that faces the camera, and fade the floor into the haze.
- **Mouse panel (61.6–66):** two small blocks in a starfield still read as toy dioramas.
  - **Fix:** fill about 60 % of the panel width with the blocks, and give them the main section's light and depth of field.
- **Motion blur:** slow pushes get almost none, as stated. This is not a blocker by itself.
