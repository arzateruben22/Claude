## Blocker: (h), four objects that still read as default

1. **Follicle pillars (5.6–39 and the fetal block).** These are plain brown cylinders with flat, lit caps, like pipes. They are the most obviously default objects in the section now.
   - **Fix:** taper them, round their tops into a bulb at the base, and give them a sheath texture and the same rim and grain as the collagen. Or sink them so only their sides show.
2. **The glass card over the micrograph (5.6–39).** A slanted translucent rectangle with a hard straight edge cuts across the upper third of the frame; it is clearest in the 10.0 still.
   - **Fix:** feather the card's edges into the haze, or drop the plane and grade the photo back with depth of field so it reads as background, not as a layer.
3. **Grain on the bundles (whole section).** At 1080 the fibril grooves read as regular brown stripes, like wood grain or ribbon, rather than fibrils.
   - **Fix:** lower the colour contrast of the stripes (keep them in the normal map rather than the albedo), vary their width, and let the sheen show the grooves.
4. **The rope (34.5–38.3).** It is a plain, smooth grey twisted solid with no fibre detail, and the missing fifth strand reads only as a faint groove at 540.
   - **Fix:** give the rope the collagen's fibre surface. Make the missing strand an obvious gap, with frayed ends either side, so "one strand in five" can be seen.

The fainter rim at 540 is not a blocker. Raise it slightly if it costs nothing.
