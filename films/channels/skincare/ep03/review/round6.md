## Blockers, in priority order

**A. 62.0–66.0: the mouse panel collides with its own badge and caption.**
- The "Model, not a photo" badge sits on the lower block, the "no scar" mice.
- The caption covers the bottom half of that same block.
- The picture that proves the line is the one you can't see.
- **Fix:**
  - Scale both blocks to about 70 % and raise them about 60 px, so the lower block ends above y ≈ 640.
  - Put "Model, not a photo" on the top row with "Mouse study", or drop it on this panel; the mouse badge already says what it is.

**B. (a) is below 4: the hook's scar band, 0–4.7 (and its return at 59.6–61).**
- **Fix:** give the band the same look v3 material as the skin around it: a satiny sheen with a slight terminator, a faint lengthwise striation, a raised edge where it meets the furrowed skin, and no pores or hairs inside it.
- Keep the glide, but make it visibly travel along the scar in the first second.

**C. (h) is below 4: the section blocks, 5.6–39, 39.3–51.5 and 61.5–66.**
- **Materials:** the collagen is still uniform smooth tubes with plasticine shading, with no fibre striation on close shots. The patch is identical white bars. The mouth block is the same weave tinted pink.
- **Lighting:** there is no visible rim on the block edges, and the slab still floats over a flat dark void with a hard edge.
- **Lens:** depth of field is shallow only at the far edge; the foreground is pin-sharp, and moves have no motion blur.
- **Mouse panel:** the blocks are small toy dioramas in a starfield.
- **Fix:**
  - Fibrillar surface detail on the collagen bundles, with a slight sheen.
  - Thinner, irregular strands in the patch.
  - A rim on the slab edges, with the slab sitting in haze so it does not float on a void.
  - Foreground focus falloff and motion blur on the pushes.
  - A distinct material for the mouth lining: wetter, finer, with no dermal pillars.
