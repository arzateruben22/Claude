## Blockers

1. **55.0–60.6 s: the real micrograph is shown under a mm scale it doesn't have (honesty).** The photo fills the frame behind ticks from 0 to 5 mm, the "10 sheets × 100 µm" stack and the depth columns.
   - At that scale the photo's nuclei measure about 100–200 µm long and its fibre bundles about 0.5 mm. Real nuclei are about 10 µm, so the photo is shown roughly 10–20 times too large.
   - The figure is labelled "true scale", and a viewer will read the photo as true scale too.
   - Fix (any one):
     - (a) Add "not to scale" to the note: "behind: a tissue photo (normal skin), not to scale".
     - (b) Defocus and desaturate the photo under the figure so it reads as texture.
     - (c) Scale the photo to its true field (a 10× field is about 1–2 mm across) and let the pull-back reveal its edge.
2. **52.3–60.6 s: the tissue-photo note sits at y ≈ 862 px, inside the bottom unsafe band (770–960 px).** Platform UI (caption and buttons) will cover it, which leaves the photo undisclosed on a phone.
   - Fix: move it into the safe box, for example at y ≈ 740 above the caption plate, or top left under the "Estimate" badge.

Small layout issues (not blockers):
- At 55–58.5 s the "0 mm" tag sits under the "Estimate" badge (it reads "0 r"). Hide it, or move the badge to the right.
- The mm ticks sit at about x 30–40, outside the 45 px side margin. Move them in about 15 px.
- At 54.4 s the cut-face section appears as a hard-edged band across the top of the photo during the push. Feather the seam.

With 1 and 2 fixed, the film passes.
