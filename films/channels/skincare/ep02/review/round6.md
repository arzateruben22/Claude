## Blockers for finals

1. **39.7–52.3 s: "face changes colour with every beat" shows no flush.**
   - Measured on a skin patch, frame by frame: from 44.6 to 49.2 s the colour holds at RGB ≈ 133/95/123 ±1. The only jumps (44.3 s, 49.3 s) are single steps, not a rhythm.
   - The line says the change is too faint to see, but the script's shot is "amplified". As it stands the picture shows nothing, and the beat is a 12.5 s hold.
   - Fix:
     - A clearly amplified, labelled flush ("amplified ×N, illustration") at about 1 Hz, in phase with the trace's peaks, from "changes colour" (41.5 s) on.
     - A slow push into the cheek across the beat.
     - Trim the hold after "in it." (49.5–52.3 s) to about 1 s.
2. **40.0–51.8 s: viewfinder text sits on the skin with no plate.** "REC DAYLIGHT" (cyan) and "MODEL SKIN · NOT A PHOTO" (lilac), each about 11 px, are drawn straight on beige skin. The contrast is about 1.5–2:1 and the honesty label is effectively unreadable.
   - Fix: put both on the HUD plate at 14 px or more, or give them a dark scrim. This is the honesty label for the beat, so it must be legible.
3. **60.9–65.6 s: "Film faces made by computer" shows two spheres** that read as eggs on violet, not faces. The idea (light that never goes inside reads as plastic) lands less well than with faces.
   - Fix: the same shader on a simple face or cheek form (brow, nose and cheek relief), still one tone and labelled "models, not photos".
   - The labels "light stays on top" / "light goes inside" are about 10–11 px; raise them to at least 14 px.
4. **(h), all macro shots (0–9, 9–18, 38.3–52.3, 66–69 s):**
   - one repeating diamond tile and uniform straight hairs;
   - a flat violet strip as the sky (0–1 s, 9–16 s);
   - the dry half of the split is still a translucent rectangle laid over the frame (9–17 s);
   - a single soft key, no rim, no volumetrics in the beam (it reads as a frosted rod at 66.0 s);
   - no motion blur.

**Where v3 and the HUD system would move (h):**
- Look v3 (a warm terminator, oily micro-speculars, tapered hairs with a dark root and backlit tip, finer grain) fixes the macro skin. That is about 45 % of the runtime, including the hook, so it is also what (a) needs. With the HUD system (one plate, one radius, the viewfinder labels on plates) I would expect (h) to reach **3**.
- To reach **4** it also needs:
  - the section re-lit with volumetric light along the paths, no visible violet backdrop, and the striped band and dash artefacts fixed;
  - the depth figures drawn in the section rather than on a card;
  - the computer-face forms;
  - a rim light and haze on the beam;
  - motion blur on the camera moves;
  - the split's dry half done as a real material change, not an overlay plate.
- Without those, the section and chart (about 25 s) keep (h) at 3.
