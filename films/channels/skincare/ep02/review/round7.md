## Blockers, in priority order

1. **(h) to 4.** In order of return:
   - **(a) Macro skin, 0–18 s and 66–69 s:** break the regular tile. Use a macro skin micro-relief with irregular cells, pores of varied size and depth, and fine secondary furrows. This is the hook frame, so it also lifts (a).
   - **(b) Section, 19.5–38 s:**
     - cut the thread count by about half and lower their brightness, so the cells read through;
     - let a handful of hero threads carry each line ("slips in", "back out", "past blood");
     - at 27–30 s, dim the threads so the false shine reads;
     - replace the violet starfield above the surface with a soft out-of-focus skin surface or warm haze.
   - **(c) Depth chart, 52.3–60.7 s:** draw the blue and red penetration inside the section (bars or light columns at true depth) instead of on a card.
   - **(d) Split, 9–17 s:** make the dry half a real material change (rougher relief, matte), not an overlay plate.
   - **(e)** Add motion blur on the camera moves.
2. **60.9–65.6 s, faces.** Sampled at 63 s, the left ("plastic") face averages about RGB 196/148/113 and the right ("glow") face about 215/162/130. The left reads tan-brown and the right peach-pink, which again lets a viewer read two skin tones paired with "plastic" and "glow".
   - Fix: match their average lightness (fill on the plastic face, or expose the two to the same mid-tone) so only the highlight and the subsurface glow differ.
   - Refine the sculpt: soft eye sockets, a nose without the dark slit, lips, and a less egg-shaped head, so the faces read as faces rather than as uncanny.
3. **(a) Hook, 0–2.8 s:** shrink the title to the series line plus one line, or bring it in at about 2 s, so the question and the image own the first 2 s.
4. **49.5–52.3 s:** trim the hold after "in it." to about 1 s, and start the flush on "beat:" (43.4 s). That lifts (f).
5. **38.3–39.7 s:** drop the section badge from the rise to the surface, or end it at the cut out of the section.
