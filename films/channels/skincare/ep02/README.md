# Skin Sense No. 002 (reboot): "Where a glow comes from"

About 70–77 s, 9:16 (1080×1920, 30 fps), narrated by a synthetic voice (bf_isabella, labelled "Narration: synthetic
voice (AI)"), with an original score (`outerlayer-slide`). The reboot's look (bible §2): UV-camera reveals and macro

**The story:** Only about 4–7% of light bounces off skin's surface as a glint (hydrated skin sends it back in one direction, dry skin scatters it); the rest goes in, scatters among cells and fibres, and much of it comes back out (≈ 40% in one worked example), tinted by melanin and blood; people asked to make faces look healthy raised blood colour (Stephen et al., 2009); blue light gets ≈ 1 mm in, red 4–5 mm.
