# Outer Layer season 1 (v2): order and tease chain

All six scripts pass the lint, warnings included:
`python3 -I kits/cinema/script/lint.py films/channels/skincare/s1v2/<topic> --segment toscale`.
Every claim was checked from search excerpts only, because the proxy blocks fetching source pages. A person opens
each link, and a board-certified dermatologist signs off every episode, before anything is posted.

The finale's tease points at **bath fingers**, a real season-2 topic. It was #3 in the original bible (§7), and it
is well sourced:
- Lewis & Pickering 1935: a finger whose nerve has been cut doesn't wrinkle.
- Kareklas et al. 2013 found that wrinkled fingers handled wet objects faster; Haseleu et al. 2014 couldn't repeat
  it, so the film would badge it as debated.

The alternative for season 2 is "Why we wrinkle", which was weighed in `SIXTH.md`.

Each tease answers the episode it follows and asks the next one's hook question, so the next episode opens on the
question its viewer was just left with.

## For the coordinator and the dermatologist

- **Episode numbers:** the end cards carry No. 001–006 in the order above; rename them if the order changes.
- **The first builder's notes:** the teases and end cards changed in every script; nothing else in the scripts did.
- **The open questions per topic** are in each `research.md`. The ones that decide wording:
  - **tanning-beds:** the UVA figure ("may be ten to fifteen times" the midday sun, AAP 2011), against WHO's "at
    least as intense as midday tropical sunlight".
  - **sunscreen-decade:** whether "about half, in small numbers" fairly describes the melanoma result
    (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.24–1.02).
  - **scar:** the 80% strength figure is from rat skin (Levenson 1965), badged on screen.
  - **botox:** the dose comparison ("well under a hundredth"): about 0.7% counted in units, about 0.1–0.2% by
    mass.
  - **glow:** no measured number ties hydration directly to gloss, so the script describes it without one.
  - **pimples:** "weeks before you see it" was dropped as poorly sourced; the script says "long before".

## Facts pass 2 (owner's direction, 2 Nov): hook, whoa, closing twist

The scripts and ledgers are in `epNN/`, re-voiced and lint clean. The hook column in the order table above dates from before this pass.

| No. | Hook | Whoa | Closing twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Pimples | Is this spot new? Its fight began under skin that looked perfectly clear. | White cells flood in until the follicle's wall can give way, spilling the fight deeper. Each day, your marrow makes several for every person on Earth. | Is it new? No: a fight begun out of sight, against a germ that even helps build your skin's barrier. |
| 02 Glow | Where's your glow from? Most light on your face goes inside you. | Blood moves with your heart, so your face changes colour with every beat: too faint to see, but an ordinary camera can count your pulse in it. | Film faces made by computer once looked like plastic: their light never went inside. Yours does; that's your glow. |
| 03 Scar | Why do you scar now? Before birth, a cut healed without one. | Until about the sixth month of pregnancy, a baby's skin regrows the weave, follicles and all. And one place in you still nearly can: your mouth. | So, why the scar? Mice born without germ-fighting cells heal without one: a scar may be the price of defence. |
| 04 Botox | Poison, in a frown? In theory, a banknote's weight could kill a million. | Its dose is counted in units, measured by their effect on mice. One frown takes twenty, under a hundredth of what could kill a person. | Poison in a frown: one message silenced, and months later the nerve grows spare endings, then prunes them away. |
| 05 Sunscreen | Which hand aged? In four and a half years, one showed no detectable ageing. | And they were followed for ten more years. Melanoma, the most serious skin cancer, came out at about half, in numbers too small to be sure. | So, which hand aged? Both groups missed mornings; one missed far fewer, and the skin kept count. |
| 06 Tanning beds | Same list as cigarettes? Processed meat is on it too. | Whole countries acted: Australia switched off every commercial bed in the land, and Brazil banned them too. | The sun made that list years before the beds. Same list as cigarettes: not the same harm, the same certainty. |
