# Outer Layer season 1 (v2): order and tease chain

All six scripts pass the lint, warnings included:
`python3 -I kits/cinema/script/lint.py films/channels/skincare/s1v2/<topic> --segment toscale`.
Every claim was checked from search excerpts only, because the proxy blocks fetching source pages. A person opens
each link, and a board-certified dermatologist signs off every episode, before anything is posted.

## Order

| No. | Topic | Hook (spoken by 2 s) | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | `pimples`: "The fight you never saw" | "Is this spot *new*? It started long before you could see it." | The widest audience (almost everyone has had a spot), and a twist in the first 2 s: the spot is the last scene of a fight that began under clear skin. Strongest hook, so it opens the season. |
| 002 | `glow`: "Where a glow comes from" | "Where does a *glow* come from? Follow the light in." | The lightest, most visual episode after a battle: pure optics, no warnings. |
| 003 | `scar`: "What's in a scar" | "Is a scar still *skin*? Look closer, thread by thread." | Turns from light to structure. Its fact (a baby's skin heals without a scar until about the sixth month) is the season's biggest "whoa" in the middle. |
| 004 | `botox`: "One message, silenced" | "What does it switch off? Not the skin, but one *message*." | The curiosity peak: a famous poison, mechanism only. Follows the scar's collagen with nerve and muscle. |
| 005 | `sunscreen-decade`: "The record under the skin" | "Which hand *aged*? Both belong to neighbours, under the same fierce sun." | A trial story, with ten years of results, that sets up the finale's evidence argument. |
| 006 | `tanning-beds`: "Same list as cigarettes" | "Same list as *cigarettes*? Most people hear that and think: just as deadly." | The highest stakes and the sharpest reframe ("not the same size of harm, but the same certainty"), so it ends the season. |

The finale's tease points at **bath fingers**, a real season-2 topic. It was #3 in the original bible (§7), and it
is well sourced:
- Lewis & Pickering 1935: a finger whose nerve has been cut doesn't wrinkle.
- Kareklas et al. 2013 found that wrinkled fingers handled wet objects faster; Haseleu et al. 2014 couldn't repeat
  it, so the film would badge it as debated.

The alternative for season 2 is "Why we wrinkle", which was weighed in `SIXTH.md`.

Each tease answers the episode it follows and asks the next one's hook question, so the next episode opens on the
question its viewer was just left with.

## For the coordinator and the dermatologist

- **Episode numbers:** the end cards carry No. 001–006 in the order above; rename them if the order changes.
- **The first builder's notes:** the teases and end cards changed in every script; nothing else in the scripts did.
- **The open questions per topic** are in each `research.md`. The ones that decide wording:
  - **tanning-beds:** the UVA figure ("may be ten to fifteen times" the midday sun, AAP 2011), against WHO's "at
    least as intense as midday tropical sunlight".
  - **sunscreen-decade:** whether "about half, in small numbers" fairly describes the melanoma result
    (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.24–1.02).
  - **scar:** the 80% strength figure is from rat skin (Levenson 1965), badged on screen.
  - **botox:** the dose comparison ("well under a hundredth"): about 0.7% counted in units, about 0.1–0.2% by
    mass.
  - **glow:** no measured number ties hydration directly to gloss, so the script describes it without one.
  - **pimples:** "weeks before you see it" was dropped as poorly sourced; the script says "long before".
