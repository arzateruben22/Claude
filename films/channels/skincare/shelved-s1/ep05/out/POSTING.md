Same cells, every colour | Outer Layer No. 005

**Description**

```
Which has more pigment cells? Count them, row by row.

So why would the work differ? Nina Jablonski and George Chaplin set skin colour beside sunlight, all around the world, and the two matched. So, which has more? None of them. Same cells, every colour, each answering its own sky.

Series: Season 1: The organ you live inside, part 5 of 6.

Sources
1. Melanocytes (the pigment cells) sit in the basal (bottom) layer of the epidermis and pass melanin to the keratinocytes around them. — histology.leeds.ac.uk: https://histology.leeds.ac.uk/skin/pigmentation.php
2. One melanocyte supplies melanin to about 36 (30–40) keratinocytes through its dendrites: the epidermal melanin unit. — altmeyers.org: https://www.altmeyers.org/en/dermatology/epidermal-melanin-unit-159334
3. On the same body site, people of every skin colour have about the same number of melanocytes per unit area of epidermis. — library.scconline.org: https://library.scconline.org/cdn-1708625391969/Melanocyte-System-Keratinization.pdf
4. In more deeply pigmented skin, melanocytes produce more melanin, with a higher share of the dark brown-black eumelanin. — NCBI (NIH): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459156/
5. Melanosomes are largest in dark skin, and in dark skin they are mostly distributed singly in keratinocytes, whereas in light skin they are mostly in clusters. — scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw: https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/handle/123456789/304455
6. Jablonski and Chaplin (2000) compared the skin reflectance of indigenous populations with remotely sensed (satellite) UV levels and found skin colour tracks UV radiation and latitude closely. — NIH: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10896812/
7. Epidermal melanocyte density averages about 955 per mm² across body sites (meta-analysis); at that density, neighbouring melanocytes are about 32 µm apart. — vivo.weill.cornell.edu: https://vivo.weill.cornell.edu/display/pubid28716435
8. A sheet of ordinary 80 gsm copy paper is about 100 µm thick. — resource.download.wjec.co.uk: https://resource.download.wjec.co.uk/vtc/2016-17/16-17_1-4/website/category/5/1papers_and_boards/category_and_standards/index.html
```
