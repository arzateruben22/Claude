The dead layer | Outer Layer No. 001

**Description**

```
Is your skin alive? The part you can see isn't: not one cell.

It looks like waste, waiting to flake away. But sealed together with fats, those shells build a kind of brick wall, and the wall keeps your water inside. So, is your skin alive? Underneath, yes. On top, it wears a layer that died for it.

Series: Season 1: The organ you live inside, part 1 of 6.

Sources
1. The outermost layer of the epidermis, the stratum corneum, is made of corneocytes: flattened, dead (cornified) keratinocytes. It is the skin's visible surface. — NCBI (NIH): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513299/
2. Corneocytes are dead, anucleate cells filled with keratin filaments inside a cross-linked protein shell (the cornified envelope). — Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/nrm1619
3. Keratinocytes are produced by cell division in the basal layer (stratum basale) of the epidermis. — bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu: https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=111965
4. The stratum corneum is commonly modelled as 'bricks and mortar': corneocytes (bricks) in a matrix of intercellular lipids (mortar). — NIH: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7592940/
5. Rutter and Hull (1979) measured skin water loss in 78 babies of 26–41 weeks' gestation; they linked the high losses of the most preterm babies to a thin, poorly keratinised stratum corneum (shown on screen: 'Rutter & Hull, 1979'). — NIH: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1545609/
6. Mean apparent stratum corneum thickness on the cheek: 16.8 µm (confocal Raman, 15 healthy Japanese adults); volar forearm 22.6 µm, back of hand 29.3 µm, palm 173 µm. — medicaljournals.se: https://medicaljournals.se/acta/content/abstract/10.2340/00015555-0183
7. Standard 80 gsm copy paper is about 100 µm thick; 17 µm is about 0.17 of it ('thinner than a fifth', computed). — resource.download.wjec.co.uk: https://resource.download.wjec.co.uk/vtc/2016-17/16-17_1-4/website/category/5/1papers_and_boards/category_and_standards/index.html
```
