# The like / follow moment (owner's request, 10 Nov 2026)

Every video gets a quick, natural, enthusiastic "like and follow/subscribe" moment that feels like the narrator
talking, not an ad read. It applies to all four active channels, and to everything still being made for Season 2.

## Where it goes
- **Shorts (60-75 s):** one moment of about 2-3 s, placed **right after the main payoff** (the "whoa" beat), at about
  70-85% of the runtime. Never in the first 20 s, and never before the hook has paid off: an early ask makes people
  swipe away.
- **Long videos:** two moments. A quick interruption after the first big payoff (about 2-3 min in), then back to the
  story. The second goes in the finale, before the end card.
- **Already-finished Season 2 shorts:** don't re-render the picture. Add the voiced line over the end card in an
  audio-only remix, with the music ducked under it, if it fits without clashing with any spoken tease. If it doesn't
  fit, leave that episode as it is and say so.

## How it sounds
- Short and in the channel's voice, tied to what just happened. Contractions, a little humour, no hype words
  ("smash", "don't forget to"), and no promises about posting schedules.
- The caption shows the line like any other line (one text block). No extra like-button graphics or arrows.
- Keep the voice-gap rule (≤1.2 s silent) and the 2-text-block rule.
- **Platform verb:** the YouTube file says "subscribe"; the TikTok/Instagram file says "follow". If the pipeline can't
  vary it per file cheaply, use "follow" in both.
- Rotate the lines below so the same one never runs on two episodes in a row.
