# Skin Sense Season 3: ASMR recording script

Thank you for lending your voice to this! Season 3 is six short ASMR lessons, each about 80 seconds: a slow, calming
journey inside the skin where the listener also learns something real. At the end of the season, the six are joined
into one longer video, which needs a few extra linking lines (Part B).

**Just under 10 minutes of reading in one session.** Every fact here is being checked against published sources. If a
line needs to change, we'll only ask you to re-read that one line.

### Your voice
- **Soft-spoken, close and unhurried.** A light whisper is welcome, as long as every word stays clear (captions are always on).
- **Slower than feels natural.** Let each line settle before the next one.
- **"…" means a gentle pause** of about a second. Leave 1 to 2 seconds between numbered lines.
- **Bold words** get a little more warmth and a little more time, never more volume. Keep your loudness steady.
- The last line of each episode is the slowest and softest.

### Setting up (about 5 minutes)
- **Room:** as quiet as you can make it. Turn off fans, air conditioning and anything that hums. A bedroom or a closet full of clothes is perfect; a bathroom or kitchen echoes.
- **Phone:** the voice memo app is fine. Put it on **Do Not Disturb** so nothing buzzes. Hold it or rest it **about 10 cm (a hand's width) from your mouth, off to one side**, so your breath doesn't hit the mic. Keep it in the same spot and don't touch it while you read.
- **Before you start:** take a sip of water, then record **10 seconds of silence**. It helps us clean up the sound.
- **Stumbled?** Pause, then read **that whole line again**. We keep the last take.
- **One file is fine:** softly say "Episode one", "Episode two" and so on before each part.
- **When you're done:** send the recording back the same way you received this script.

## Part A. The six lessons

### Episode 1: What One Shiver Does Inside You

**1.** Feel that? … A shiver.

**2.** Tiny sensors in your skin feel the air cool… and quietly let your **brain** know.

**3.** Your brain answers in two soft steps.

**4.** First, the little blood vessels near your skin **narrow**. Warm blood stays deeper inside you.

**5.** Then, if you're still cold… your muscles begin to **tremble**. Not on purpose. Tiny, quick tightenings, over and over.

**6.** They do just one thing. They make **heat**. A strong shiver makes several times more than your body makes at rest.

**7.** And goosebumps? The same signal reaches the tiny muscle beside each hair… and lifts it **upright**.

**8.** One cool breeze. Skin, nerves, vessels and muscle, all answering together. All… **without asking you**.

### Episode 2: Where Do Your Skin Cells Go?

**1.** Deep in your skin, right now, a new cell is being **born**.

**2.** It starts at the very bottom… and slowly begins to **rise**.

**3.** As it climbs, it fills with a tough protein called **keratin**. It grows flatter… and flatter.

**4.** Near the top, it lets go of its nucleus… and its life gently ends.

**5.** So the skin you can touch right now is made of cells that have **finished their lives**.

**6.** Stacked like bricks, they seal water in.

**7.** The whole journey takes about a **month**.

**8.** Then, quietly, the top cells drift away, far too small to see. Some of the dust in your home… began as **you**.

**9.** Underneath, new cells are already rising. Always rebuilding… from the **bottom up**.

### Episode 3: What Is Actually Inside a Pore?

**1.** Look closely at your skin. Those tiny dots… are **pores**.

**2.** The pores you can see are little doorways, into narrow tunnels called hair **follicles**.

**3.** Even where you can't see one… a fine hair is usually growing inside.

**4.** Partway down sits a small **oil gland**. Its cells do something strange.

**5.** Each one slowly fills with oil… until the whole cell breaks down and **becomes** oil.

**6.** That oil is called **sebum**. It glides up along the hair, and spreads over your skin in a thin, soft coat.

**7.** Tiny **microbes** live in there too, quietly feeding on that oil.

**8.** That's a pore. A doorway. A hair. A little gland. And its tiny residents.

**9.** Until… the doorway gets **blocked**.

### Episode 4: How One Pore Turns Into a Pimple

**1.** A pimple begins deep inside a pore… long before you see **anything**.

**2.** Usually, old cells drift up and out with the oil.

**3.** But sometimes… they **cling together** instead. Oil and cells pack into a tiny plug. And the doorway starts to close.

**4.** If the top stays open, the plug can darken in the air. That's a **blackhead**. And that dark color… isn't dirt.

**5.** If skin closes over it, it stays pale.

**6.** Behind the plug, oil keeps arriving, with nowhere to go. The pore slowly **swells**.

**7.** The microbes inside have a feast.

**8.** And your body notices. Immune cells gather, and the spot turns red, warm and **swollen**.

**9.** That's the pimple you finally see. A small, blocked doorway… and your body, working to **clear it**.

### Episode 5: Every Hair on You Is Scheduled to Fall Out

**1.** Every hair on your body is quietly keeping **time**.

**2.** It grows. It pauses. It rests. And then… it lets **go**.

**3.** On your head, growing can last for **years**. That's why it can grow so long.

**4.** On your eyebrows, it lasts only **months**. So they stay short.

**5.** When growing ends, the root shrinks and the hair loosens. Then it rests, for a couple of months.

**6.** Meanwhile, in the same follicle… a **brand-new hair** begins, and gently pushes the old one out.

**7.** So losing fifty to a hundred hairs a day, from your head alone, is normal.

**8.** Every follicle keeps its **own schedule**, so they never all let go at once.

**9.** Right now, you're losing hair… and growing it… at the **same time**.

### Episode 6: Your Skin Is Still Healing After It Looks Healed

**1.** When a small gap in your skin closes, it looks finished in a week or two. But it **isn't**.

**2.** Underneath… the work has barely begun.

**3.** First, your body seals it, and calls in its cleaning crews.

**4.** New cells creep in from the edges, and repair cells lay down fresh **collagen**. Quickly… a little messily… like scaffolding.


**5.** But for months, sometimes a year or more, the skin keeps quietly **rebuilding**.

**6.** Thin, quick collagen is traded for stronger fibers, drawn slowly into **line**. The spot grows flatter… softer… paler.

**7.** Even then, rebuilt skin usually ends up at most around **eighty percent** as strong as before.

**8.** So the skin you think of as healed… is still being gently **rewoven**. Long after you've forgotten it.
