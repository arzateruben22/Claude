# Skin Sense Season 3: recording script

Thank you for lending your voice to this! Season 3 is six short lessons, each about 80 seconds, that take the
viewer on a slow, beautiful journey inside the skin. At the end of the season, the six are joined into one longer
video, which needs a few extra linking lines (Part B).

**About 9 minutes of reading in one session.** Every fact here is being checked against published sources. If a line
needs to change, we'll only ask you to re-read that one line.

### How to record
- **Room:** quiet, with soft things around you. A bedroom or a closet full of clothes is perfect; a bathroom or kitchen echoes.
- **Phone:** the voice memo app is fine. Hold it **about a hand's width from your mouth**, slightly to the side. Same spot for the whole session.
- **Pauses:** leave 2 seconds between lines. Stumbled? Pause, then read **that whole line again**. We keep the last take.
- **One file is fine:** say "Episode one", "Episode two" and so on before each part, so we can find them.
- **When you're done:** send the recording back the same way you received this script.

### How it should sound
Soft and close, like you're explaining something lovely to one person in a quiet room. Slow, warm and unhurried. Let
the pauses breathe. Speak softly, but don't whisper: every word should still be clear on a phone speaker.
- **Bold words** are the heart of the line: give them a little extra care.
- The last line of each episode lands gently, a little slower.

## Part A. The six lessons

### Episode 1: What One Shiver Does Inside You

**1.** Feel that? A shiver.

**2.** It starts before you even notice the cold. Sensors in your skin feel the temperature drop, and they send word to your **brain**.

**3.** Your brain answers in two ways.

**4.** First, the small blood vessels near your skin **narrow**, keeping warm blood deeper inside you. That's why cold fingers turn pale.

**5.** Then, if that isn't enough, your muscles start to **shake**. Not on purpose. Tiny, fast tightenings, over and over, that do almost nothing except one thing: they make **heat**.

**6.** A hard shiver can make several times more heat than your body makes at rest.

**7.** And remember goosebumps? The same cold signal reaches the tiny muscle beside each hair, and pulls it **upright**.

**8.** One cold breeze. Skin, nerves, vessels and muscle, all answering together. All **without asking you**.

### Episode 2: Where Do Your Skin Cells Go?

**1.** Right now, deep in your skin, a cell is being **born**.

**2.** It starts at the very bottom of the outer layer, and it begins to **rise**.

**3.** As it climbs, it changes. It fills with a tough protein called **keratin**. It flattens out.

**4.** And near the top, it gives up its nucleus, and its life ends.

**5.** That's the surprise. The surface of your skin, the part you can touch, is made of cells that have **finished their lives**.

**6.** Stacked like bricks, they form a seal that keeps water in.

**7.** The whole journey, bottom to top, takes about a **month**.

**8.** Then, quietly, the top cells let go. You shed many thousands of them every minute, far too small to see. Some of the dust in your home started as **you**.

**9.** And underneath, new cells are already rising to take their place. Your skin is always being rebuilt, from the **bottom up**.

### Episode 3: What Is Actually Inside a Pore?

**1.** Look closely at your skin, and you'll see tiny dots. **Pores**.

**2.** But a pore isn't a hole into nothing. The pores you can see are doorways, each one opening into a narrow tunnel called a hair **follicle**.

**3.** Even where you can't see a hair, there's usually a fine one growing inside.

**4.** Partway down the tunnel sits a small **oil gland**. Its cells do something strange.

**5.** Each one slowly fills with oil, until the whole cell breaks apart and **becomes** oil.

**6.** That oil is called **sebum**. It flows up the tunnel, along the hair, and spreads across your skin as a thin, soft coat.

**7.** And the tunnel isn't empty in another way. Tiny **microbes** live in there, quietly, feeding on that oil.

**8.** For most of your life, that's all a pore is. A doorway, a hair, a little gland, and its tiny residents.

**9.** Until the doorway gets **blocked**.

### Episode 4: How One Pore Turns Into a Pimple

**1.** A pimple doesn't start on the surface. It starts deep inside a pore, long before you see **anything**.

**2.** Normally, the cells lining the pore shed and drift up and out with the oil.

**3.** But sometimes they **stick together** instead. Oil and cells pack into a tiny plug, and the doorway begins to close.

**4.** If the plug stays open at the top, it can turn dark in the air. That's a **blackhead**, and the dark color isn't dirt.

**5.** If skin closes over it, it stays pale.

**6.** Behind the plug, oil keeps coming, with nowhere to go. The pore **swells**.

**7.** The microbes that live there have more food than ever.

**8.** And your body notices. Immune cells rush in, and the area turns red, warm and **swollen**.

**9.** That's the pimple you finally see. A small, blocked doorway, and your body working to **clear it**.

### Episode 5: Every Hair on You Is Scheduled to Fall Out

**1.** Every hair on your body is on a **timer**.

**2.** It grows. It stops. It rests. And then it lets **go**.

**3.** On your head, the growing phase can last for **years**. That's why the hair on your head can grow so long.

**4.** On your eyebrows, it lasts only **months**, so they stay short.

**5.** When growing ends, the root shrinks, and the hair loosens from the cells that fed it. Then it rests for a couple of months, held loosely in place.

**6.** Meanwhile, deep in the same follicle, a **brand-new hair** starts to grow. And when it's ready, it pushes the old one out.

**7.** That's why losing hair every day is normal. Often fifty to a hundred from your head alone.

**8.** You barely notice, because every follicle keeps its **own schedule**. They never all let go at once.

**9.** You're losing hair right now. And growing it, at the **same time**.

### Episode 6: Your Skin Is Still Healing After It Looks Healed

**1.** When a small gap in your skin closes, it looks finished in a week or two. It **isn't**.

**2.** Underneath, the work has barely started.

**3.** First, your body seals the gap and calls in its cleaning crews.

**4.** Then new cells crawl in from the edges to bridge it, and repair cells lay down fresh **collagen**. Quickly, and a little messily, like scaffolding.

**5.** That's when the surface looks healed.

**6.** But for months afterward, sometimes a year or more, the skin keeps **rebuilding**.

**7.** The quick, thin collagen is slowly swapped for stronger fibers. Loose threads are pulled into **line**. The spot grows flatter, softer, paler.

**8.** Even then, it doesn't fully return to how it was. Rebuilt skin usually ends up at most around **eighty percent** as strong as before.

**9.** So the skin you see as healed is still a work in progress. Quietly being **rewoven**, long after you've forgotten about it.

## Part B. The linking lines for the long video

These join the six lessons into one longer video. Read them in order.

**1. Opening.** Your skin is never still. Right now it's shedding, rebuilding, growing hair and tending every pore. These are six quiet stories from inside it. Get comfortable.

**2. After episode 1.** A shiver is your skin answering in seconds. Some of its work takes a whole **month**.

**3. After episode 2.** Those cells rise all around the tiny doorways that cover your skin. So what's inside one?

**4. After episode 3.** And when one of those doorways gets blocked...

**5. After episode 4.** Every pore holds a hair. And every hair is running on a **schedule**.

**6. After episode 5.** Hair renews itself on a timer. Skin renews itself too, long after it looks done.

**7. Closing.** So that's your skin, working quietly. It shivers to keep you warm. It rebuilds itself from the bottom up. It oils, it sheds, it grows, and it repairs, for months after you've stopped looking. And almost none of it asks for your attention. Thank you for spending these few minutes inside it.
