   - Ants and people are weighed as dry carbon (badged "∵ weighed as carbon (dry)" 34.3–47.5 s), the unit both
     censuses use, so no conversion factor is assumed. The beam leans in proportion to the ratio (14° to the ants at
     "twice", 25° to us at "a fifth"), not all the way as a real balance would.
   - The carpet in "each" is an idealised square grid of ants 5 mm apart (4 per cm², lightly jittered), 2,439,844
     specks in a 781 cm square round one 170 cm figure. It is to scale, but real ants are neither evenly spaced nor
     gathered round one person; the spine rounds it to "≈ 2.5 million ants".

     labelled "a worker ant, a few mm". Worker size varies widely between species; check "a few mm" reads as typical.
   - The second balance (ants against "every wild bird and mammal") leans only 9° toward the ants, on purpose: c5
     says only that ants' biomass "exceeds" theirs, and no ratio is claimed.
