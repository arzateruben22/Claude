**Description**

```
How long to walk to the Moon? Starting at your own front door.

The Moon is about 30 Earths away. At an easy 5 km/h, never stopping, you would arrive in about 9 years. Walk 8 hours a day and it is about 26 years. At 10,000 steps a day, the popular goal, it is about 140 years. Apollo 11's crew made the same trip in about three days: the distance never changed, only the speed.

Series: Season 2, episode 1 of 6.

Sources
1. The Moon's mean distance from Earth is about 384,400 km (shown on screen as '384,400 km'; the Moon's orbit is elliptical, 356,400-406,700 km). — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
2. 384,400 km / 12,756 km (Earth's equatorial diameter) = 30.1: 'about thirty Earths'. — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
3. An easy walking pace is about 5 km/h (about 1.4 m/s); comfortable gait speed in healthy adults is roughly 1.3-1.4 m/s. — pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9143432/
4. 384,400 km / 5 km/h = 76,880 h = 3,203 days = 8.8 years: 'about nine years' (nonstop, a thought experiment with no road, rest or air). — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
5. Assumption, not a measurement: 8 hours of walking a day (the rest eating, sleeping, resting). Shown as a green '∵ eight hours a day' badge. — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
6. 76,880 h / 8 h a day = 9,610 days = 26.3 years: 'about twenty-six years' (shown '≈ 26 yr', boxed). — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
7. 10,000 steps a day is the popular goal; at an assumed 0.75 m a step that is about 7.5 km a day (against 40 km a day at 5 km/h for 8 hours). — pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14715035/
8. At 8 h a day: 4 km/h gives about 33 years, 6 km/h about 22 (shown on the work card). — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
9. Apollo 11 reached lunar orbit about 75 h 50 min after launch (July 1969): about three days. The plural 'crews' was dropped: only Apollo 11 is sourced. — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/history/apollo-11-mission-overview/
10. 384,400 km / 7.5 km a day = 51,253 days = 140 years (calc.py: years_steps). — NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
```
