**Title**

Every shuffle is new | Possibility Lab Season 2 · Episode 4

**Description**

```
Has this ever happened? Shuffle a deck, and look at the order.

Fifty-two cards can be ordered in 52! ways: about 8.07 × 10^67, a number with 68 digits. Give everyone alive a deck, a shuffle a second, for 13.8 billion years, and that is about 3.6 × 10^27 shuffles: less than one part in 10^36 of all the orders. Shuffle well (about seven riffle shuffles) and your order has almost certainly never existed before.
```
