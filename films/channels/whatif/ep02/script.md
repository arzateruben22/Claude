## normal {shot: the square meter splits in two; a second figure joins · spine "8.2 billion × ½ m²" · picture-only 0.3 s · energy: 0.45} (5–15 s)

## change {shot: the crowd spreads outward ring by ring, the camera rising with it; a city outline drawn under it · energy: 0.6} (15–27 s)

## why {shot: the same square shrinks by half as density doubles, then turns amber-free warning hatching (chalk) and springs back · badge: ∵ 2 per m² (safe) · picture-only 2 s · energy: 0.5} (27–43 s)

## whoa {shot: pull back to the whole planet's land, the square a single marker dot; then the crowd folds into a cube beside a tower · picture-only 3 s · energy: 0.75} (43–56 s)

## takeaway {shot: back down to the one blue figure in their square meter · picture-only 0.3 s · energy: 0.45} (61–67 s)
vo: So the world isn't full. It just feels that way from inside one *city*. {c9}
