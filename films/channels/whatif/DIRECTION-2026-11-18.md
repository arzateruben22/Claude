# Possibility Lab: format direction (owner, 18 Nov 2026)

Mono keeps running the same simulation. Each short is one more notch of it: more spin (Season 3), later more gravity
(Season 4, `gravity-doubles/SEASON4.md`), more speed, more distance. No new character, no reset between parts.

- A short = one notch (Season 3: Part 1 x1.2, Part 2 x2, Part 3 x4 ...), same HUD, same look, the cliffhanger is the next notch.
- The long YouTube video = the notches of its three shorts in ONE continuous run (no reset, no recap), plus extra notches
  only if it needs the length (YouTube mid-roll ads need about 8 minutes).
- The plan is about 3 shorts per channel, then one long video per 3 shorts (owner, 18 Nov). Parts 4-8 and the 8-chapter long film
  are on hold; their configs and sfx stems stay committed.
