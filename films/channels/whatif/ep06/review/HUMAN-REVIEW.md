  report of the study (search excerpt): a hydrologist should open the paper. Other budgets: Trenberth et al. 2007
  (about 486,000, as commonly quoted) and GRID-Arendal's 577,000 km³, which is the *total water circulated*, used
  only as an upper bound (the work card says "upper bound").

- **Amazons:** the mean discharge at the mouth is about 205,000–208,000 m³/s ±6% (IRD study, search excerpt); 80 and
  7 million (6.9) follow.

  (US Mint). The box says "≈ 3 mm"; the sum shows 2.8 mm.

  (badged). The film never claims what would physically happen to air or ground under such a pour.
- **The storm column:** the WMO 24-hour record, 1,825 mm at Foc-Foc, Réunion, 7–8 January 1966 (WMO table); drawn to
  scale beside a 1.7 m figure (not to scale with the figure's own body, which is generic).
