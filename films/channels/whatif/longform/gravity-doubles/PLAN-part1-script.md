# Possibility Lab Season 3: "What If Gravity Doubled Every Hour?"
## Part 1 script + the 8-part outline (for your OK; nothing is rendered yet)

**Same art style as Season 3 so far:** the visor robot standing on top of the little glowing Earth, the same HUD, the same
magenta. What's new is a *visual meter* that shows gravity growing without words:
- **A bouncing ball.** Same throw every hour, so its bounce gets half as high each time. A dashed line marks each hour's height.
- **A big HUD:** an hour clock, a **GRAVITY ×1** dial that thuds to ×2, ×4, ×8…, and the robot's **WEIGHT** bar filling past 100%.
- **The world reacting:** knees bending, branches sagging, the air layer sinking, mountains slumping, and in the finale the
  whole planet collapsing.

**The rule we play by (said once, in Part 1):** Earth gets twice as heavy every hour. Same size, just heavier, so it pulls
twice as hard. Nothing else in the universe changes.

**A correction to what I told you earlier:** I said Earth becomes a black hole at about hour 29. That only holds if
Earth's rock could stay planet-sized, and it can't. The better (and wilder) answer:
- **Hour 18:** Earth outweighs the Sun while still about Earth-sized. That's literally a **white dwarf**.
- **About hour 19½:** nothing can hold it up any more. It collapses into a **black hole** about 14 km across.
- **About hour 40:** it's as heavy as the black hole at the centre of the Milky Way, and the Sun falls in.

All of this gets checked against sources before anything posts.
