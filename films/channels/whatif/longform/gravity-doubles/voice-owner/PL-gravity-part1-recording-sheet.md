# Possibility Lab: "What If Gravity Doubled Every Hour?" Part 1, your recording sheet

**First, watch `part1-guide.mp4`.** It's the finished picture with a placeholder voice (that voice never ships) and the
current line shown at the bottom. Your recording replaces the placeholder, timed to the same picture.

**The vibe:** playful and curious, like you're letting a friend in on something wild. Smile while you talk. Let the
numbers land.

### How to record
- **Room:** quiet, with soft things around you (a closet or bedroom).
- **Phone:** Voice Memos, a hand's width (15–20 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- **Start by saying:** "Possibility Lab, gravity, part one."
- **Pauses:** 2 seconds between lines. Flubbed one? Pause, then read **that whole line again**; I keep the last take.
- **Pace:** about the guide's pace. Each line has a time window (below). Under it is perfect. A little over is fine;
  I can trim pauses.
- **One file:** `pl-gravity-part1.m4a` (under 25 MB; if bigger, set Voice Memos to Compressed).
- **Upload:** https://github.com/arzateruben22/claude/upload/claude/whatif-s2/films/channels/whatif/longform/gravity-doubles/voice-owner-spoken
  (or attach it here in the chat).

**Bold** = lean on it. **…** = a tiny suspense pause. *[What you'll see]* isn't read aloud.
