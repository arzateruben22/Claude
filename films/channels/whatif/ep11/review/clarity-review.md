# ep11 clarity review (S2E05, folder ep11)

- Hook: "How big is Earth's water? Gather every drop into one ball, and measure it." The question lands by 1.2 s; the picture is you, a glass of water and a small ball that grows as the water leaves the glass.
- Figures spoken: one point four billion cubic kilometers, fourteen hundred kilometers, four hundred kilometers, an eighth of one percent. The screen carries 1,386 million km³, 1,383 km, 404 km, 40 % of the Moon, 11 % of the Earth, 0.13 %, the 2.5 % fresh share.
- The turn: six sources stream into one ball that grows with the counter (volume), so its size is honest; the ruler across it reads 1,400 km; the Moon (left) and the Earth (right) are drawn to scale beside it. The sources' own sizes are not to scale (badged).
- Honesty: "all water" means liquid, ice and vapor counted together (badge); the ball is a perfect sphere at one density. The thin blue film on the Earth is drawn about 30 times thicker than it is (badge, claim c8).
- Hard cuts only between scales (glass to km stage, Moon, Earth, work, takeaway, end); the end card sits on a heavily dimmed scene, and the dim lifts on the bare glass scene (the loop point).
- Open items: all claims OPEN (USGS page not read: 1.386 bn km³, 2.5 % fresh, the list of sources; NASA Moon and Earth pages for the diameters). The shape of the Earth's film is decoration.
