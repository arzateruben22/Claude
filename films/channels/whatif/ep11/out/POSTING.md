**Description**

```
How big is Earth's water? Gather every drop into one ball, and measure it.

All of Earth's water, liquid, ice and vapor together, is about 1.386 billion cubic kilometers. Squeezed into one ball, it is about 1,400 km wide: 40% of the Moon's width and 11% of the Earth's. Only the fresh water makes a ball about 400 km wide. Its share of the Earth's volume is only about an eighth of one percent.

Series: Season 2, episode 5 of 6.
```
