# ChatGPT art prompts, one per channel

How to use:
1. Open a **new ChatGPT chat for each channel** (keep one chat per channel so the look stays consistent).
2. Paste that channel's **STEP 1** once. Wait for "ready".
3. For each picture, paste the **shot template** with the blanks filled in (or just paste the shot lines Claude sends you for an episode).
4. Download each image, name it `<channel>-ep<NN>-<shot>.png` (e.g. `orbit-ep08-03.png`), and send it to Claude.

All channels share these rules: no text in the image (Claude adds all words in code), no real people's faces, no logos or brands, nothing copied from another creator.

What the pictures are for:
- **Skin Sense:** the pictures *are* the episode art (as in Episodes 4-6).
- **Reality Rulebook, One More Orbit, Possibility Lab:** these episodes are animated in code. ChatGPT pictures are used as **backgrounds, props and mood references** that the builder matches in code, so ask for clean, uncluttered images with room around the subject.
