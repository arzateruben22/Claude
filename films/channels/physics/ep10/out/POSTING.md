Why does coffee leave a ring? Not blobs.

The edge of a drying drop is stuck to the paper. Water evaporates from that edge, so water from the middle flows out to replace it, and the flow carries the specks along. In our simple model, about nine in ten specks end up in the ring. Make the specks oval and the ring can vanish.
