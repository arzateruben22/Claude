# The like / follow moment (owner's request, 10 Nov 2026; every other video since 12 Nov 2026)

**Every other video** (the owner, 12 Nov 2026) gets a quick, natural "like and follow/subscribe" moment that feels
like the narrator talking, not an ad read: the odd parts of a series (Part 1, 3, 5, 7) and the odd episodes of a
season. The others get no spoken ask: only the silent thumbs-up and bell pop-up (`cta.py`, no voice) and, in a
series, the closer ("Part 2 of 8"), which is the better reason to follow. Why: in a 60-75 s short, watching to the
end and rewatching matter more than likes, and a spoken ask mid-story is a swipe-away moment; the follow button is
on screen anyway. It applies to all four active channels. Once Prism has analytics, compare the retention dip at the
ask in videos with and without it, and record the result in PACKAGING.md "What's working".

## Where it goes
- **Shorts (60-75 s):** one moment of **one sentence, about 2-3 s, no lead-in ("Hang on", "Quick pause") and no
  way back ("Okay, back to the math")**, placed **right after a payoff** (the "whoa" beat), never mid-buildup, at
  about 70-85% of the runtime. Never in the first 20 s, and never before the hook has paid off: an early ask makes people
  swipe away.
- **Long videos:** two moments. A quick interruption after the first big payoff (about 2-3 min in), then back to the
  story. The second goes in the finale, before the end card.
- **Already-finished Season 2 shorts:** don't re-render the picture. Add the voiced line over the end card in an
  audio-only remix, with the music ducked under it, if it fits without clashing with any spoken tease. If it doesn't
  fit, leave that episode as it is and say so.

## How it sounds
- Short and in the channel's voice, tied to what just happened. Contractions, a little humour, no hype words
  ("smash", "don't forget to"), and no promises about posting schedules.
- The caption shows the line like any other line (one text block). The only graphic is the owner's thumbs-up and
  bell pop-up (`cta.py`), on every video, with or without the spoken line.
- Keep the voice-gap rule (≤1.2 s silent) and the 2-text-block rule.
- **Platform verb:** the YouTube file says "subscribe"; the TikTok/Instagram file says "follow". If the pipeline can't
  vary it per file cheaply, use "follow" in both.
- Rotate the lines below so the same one never runs on two episodes in a row.
