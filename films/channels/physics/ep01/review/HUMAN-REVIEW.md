- **The bullet line is gone.** "That holds even for a bullet fired level" broke the bible's no-firearms rule (§9) and was true only without air (a fired bullet lands about 40 ms after a dropped one in air, about 24 frames at ×1/20). The takeaway now ends "In air, the throws land a sliver after the drop" (c14, the drag numbers on the badge).

- **The air badge names its reference:** "With air, the fastest throw lands 0.3 ms after the drop. One frame here is 1.7 ms." Drag slows the drop too (by 1.54 ms), so 0.3 ms is against the dropped ball, not the air-free scene.
