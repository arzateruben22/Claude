# Reality Rulebook · Season 2 long video: opening, bridges, finale (narration only; the six episodes play whole)

## opening {shot: card}
vo: A slinky. A strand of spaghetti. A jar of nuts. A coffee drop. A cup of water. And a raindrop. {-} Six ordinary things. {-} Each one hides a reason that is too quick, too small, or too hidden to see. {-} So we'll slow each one down, or speed it up, until the physics shows. {-}

## bridge1 {shot: card}
vo: Quick pause: if that one got you, tap like and subscribe. Okay, back to it. {-} The slinky's bottom waited, because the news of the drop had to travel down it, like a wave. {-} A bending stick has the same problem. {-} When it snaps, a wave from the break runs along it, and that wave can break it again. {-}

## bridge2 {shot: card}
vo: Spaghetti breaks into a few pieces. {-} Now let's watch a whole jar of pieces at once. {-} Shake it, and something strange happens to the biggest one. {-}

## bridge3 {shot: card}
vo: In the jar, small grains slipping into the gaps under the big nut is one reason it rises. {-} Small things moving together can do surprising things. {-} So can the tiny specks inside a drop of drying coffee. {-}

## bridge4 {shot: card}
vo: The coffee drop hid a flow that carries its specks to the edge. {-} Now let's listen for a different hidden shape: the empty air inside a cup. {-}

## bridge5 {shot: card}
vo: A cup's note climbs as its empty space shrinks. {-} For the last one, water is falling through the air instead, from a cloud. {-}

## finale {shot: card}
vo: A slinky that waits. A stick that breaks again. A nut that rises. A ring from a drop. A cup that sings. A raindrop with a speed limit. {-} None of them needed new physics, only a closer look. {-} And all of them were happening around you the whole time. {-} Real quick: like and subscribe. It genuinely helps. {-} Next time you see something ordinary, slow it down in your head. {-}
