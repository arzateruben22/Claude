# Reality Rulebook Season 3: the ChatGPT prompts (v2, one flowing shot per part)

fly, the mosquito and the drop are all in one shot. The trick is that **time is slowed but the camera isn't**: it glides at
a normal pace through the slowed-down kitchen (the "bullet time" look). Each clip **starts on the exact frame the clip
before it ended on**, and the speed changes are **smooth slow-downs inside the shot**, never cuts.

exactly right for 100× slower. Only the switching needs fixing, so Part 2 below is a flowing re-take.

**Still the rule: clean pictures.** No text, numbers, counters, labels or titles in the clips (Episode 2 had "100×",
"THE FLY" and captions baked in). Your voice, the captions, the speed tag and the slowing clock are added on top here,
in sync with you.
