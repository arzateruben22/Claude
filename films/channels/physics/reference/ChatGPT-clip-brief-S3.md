# Reality Rulebook Season 3: what to ask ChatGPT for (video clips, all 8 parts)

**How this works:** ChatGPT makes the realistic footage; Claude does everything else (your voice, captions, the speed
counter and slowing clock, side-by-sides, drawings like the wingtip figure-eight, sound, mix, covers, physics check).
So every clip must be **clean**: no text, no numbers, no counters, no captions. Your sample had those baked in; the
picture was perfect, so this is the same look without the words.

**The style you liked:** few words, the visuals play out. Each part opens at one slow-motion speed and **steps slower
halfway**, so the second speed shows something new. Each clip below says exactly how fast the motion should look on
screen, because ChatGPT doesn't know what "100× slower" looks like; the numbers come from the physics.

**Steps**
1. Paste the **setup** once (below).
2. Send the clips **one at a time**, in order. If a clip comes back wrong (text on it, wrong speed, extra wings), ask
   for it again.
3. Name each file as listed (`p2-c1`, `p2-c2`, …) and attach them here in the chat, or upload them to
   https://github.com/arzateruben22/claude/upload/claude/physics-s2/films/channels/physics/reference/clips
   (GitHub takes up to 25 MB a file; the chat took your 35 MB one).
4. You don't need every clip at once: one part at a time is perfect.
