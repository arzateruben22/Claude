**The science (checked before anything posts):**
- Sound travels at about 343 m/s (767 mph) in air at about 20 °C (68 °F).
- At 1,000× that's 34 cm/s (about a foot a second); at 10,000× it's 3.4 cm/s (about an inch and a third a second). Both are derivations.
- About 4 m of kitchen takes roughly 0.012 s (about a hundredth of a second).
- A clap's sound comes from the air pocket between the palms being squeezed out. *Source to open: Fu & Jung et al., "Hand clapping as a Helmholtz resonator", Physical Review Fluids (2021).*
- A hummingbird's wings beat about 50 times a second, so at 1,000× slower a single beat takes 20 s and it looks frozen.
