**The science (checked before anything posts):**
- A kernel pops at about 180 °C (356 °F), when the steam pressure cracks the hull. *Source to open: Virot & Ponomarenko, "Popcorn: critical temperature, jump and sound", J. R. Soc. Interface (2015).*
- The starch "leg" that kicks it off the pan: same paper.
- The bubble's hole edge moves at several metres a second. That comes from the soap-film "Culick speed", about 7 m/s for a film around a micrometre thick, so a 4 cm bubble pops in about a hundredth of a second (a derivation).
- The rainbow colours come from interference in a film under a micrometre thick.
