- **The people and the wrong idea:** Camillo Golgi saw the nervous system as one continuous web;
  Santiago Ramón y Cajal saw separate cells. They shared the 1906 Nobel Prize and still disagreed.
  Electron microscopes in the 1950s showed the gap.
- **The mechanism:** the signal can't cross as electricity; calcium floods the tip, vesicles of
  chemical messenger fuse and release, the messenger drifts across and starts a new signal.
- **The escalation:** the crossing takes about a millisecond, and these junctions can strengthen with
  use, one way the brain is thought to learn.
- **"Most":** electrical synapses exist; the claim and the picture are about chemical synapses.
- **Spine readout:** the gap's width and the crossing's time, then the tally.
