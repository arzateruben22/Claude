## Owner's rules

- **Red cells as real dented discs, never glossy spheres: broken.** 44.0–51.5: the lung hero cell is a convex, glossy
  capsule (a jellybean) with a hard specular streak and no dimple; the distant cells at 53.6–64.9 are the same capsules.
  The hook cell (0–9.6) and the end card (70.4) are fine (a real biconcave cut profile). The vein's cells (25–38.5) are
  flat red slivers, not discs.
- **Nothing over the series line and title while they show: broken at 2.3–2.9.** The "O₂ seats in this one cell,
  about 1,000,000,000" readout fades in over "A billion passengers" and the pulse line (2.5 s frame: the number sits on
  the pulse line and the label on the title's descender). `spine.seats.t0` = `title.t1` = 2.3167, so they crossfade.

## Findings (most important first)

1. **(h) Primitives on screen in four of six shots.** 24.4–39.6: the skin is a plain box with a gradient blue stripe,
   cut faces in flat pastel bands, and the light paths are flat 2D arrows (a textbook diagram). 65.2–70.4: myelin is a row
   of plain cylinder capsules with a flat chartreuse box at each node. 39.6–65: the air sacs are smooth beige spheres and
   the capillary is a straight glass cylinder (the look test promised a tapered branching mesh). 10–24: hemoglobin is four
   glossy gummy lobes, O₂ are white bead pairs drifting like popcorn. **Fix:** skin as a layered, wet section with
   epidermal micro-relief, SSS and a dermal capillary bed; light as volumetric beams scattering (blue diffusing out near
   the surface, red diffusing deep), not arrows. Myelin as wrapped, slightly irregular sheaths with lamellae visible at
   the cut and a soft node gap; the jump as a travelling glow, not a lit box. Air sacs as thin, translucent, crumpled walls
   from `set/scan.js`/the look-test lungs, with a tapered, branching capillary net. Hemoglobin with ribbon/surface detail,
   rougher clearcoat, and a heme glint per seat; O₂ smaller, varied, with motion blur.

2. **(owner rule, h) Glossy capsule red cells in the whoa (44.0–64.9), and the hero cell clips the vessel wall.** At 47.0
   and 49.4 the cell's lower edge passes through the capillary wall (the glint sits on the intersection); at 49.7 it pokes
   out past the tube. **Fix:** use `set/rbc.js` (Evans–Fung) folded to fit the lumen, roughness ~0.45 and a weak
   clearcoat; scale the hero cell so it fits the lumen, or widen the tube for this macro shot.

3. **(c) The whoa has no picture: the seats never visibly fill.** 46.0 and 49.4 show the same bright red cell; script
   and `lung.flush` [46.67, 49.67] promise "dark cells flush bright on contact with the air sac". The O₂ (47.0) drift
   outside the tube. Same miss earlier: by 20.6 every lobe is bright, so "Empty, it's dark red" (21.1–22.0) plays over a
   full molecule. **Fix:** make the hero cell clearly garnet on arrival (deoxy `#7A0F22`), put the air-sac wall in the
   frame, and run the flush across the cell from the wall side over 46.7–49.7 with O₂ crossing the wall into it; tie the
   counter to the flush. For 21.1, hold the lobes dark until all four are aboard, then flush on "full" (22.5), or show an
   empty twin beside it on "Empty".

4. **(owner rule) Readout over the title, 2.3–2.9.** See above. **Fix:** start the seats readout at 3.0 (after the title
   is fully out, as ep01's R4 badge fix did), or place it below y 0.30 while the title shows; add a compile-time check
   that no spine/badge/label t0 is earlier than `title.t1` + its fade.

5. **(d, glitch) Camera pop and HUD pile-up in the skin shot, 27.4–29.0.** `film.json` `cam.skin` has a keyframe at
   28.867 before one at 27.367 (`skin.section` = [28.87, 27.37], end before start), so the camera snaps from the top view
   to the side section at 28.87, mid "the blood inside is too". Around it: the 75 % plate covers the "vein, seen through
   skin" label (28.9), overlaps the "you are here" locator (28.0–29.0), and the "returning blood" label sits on the scan
   tag over empty scan view (28.0–28.8). **Fix:** swap the section times in `compile.py` (start 27.37, land 28.87, eased),
   end the "vein" label before the move, and start the 75 % plate only after the camera lands.

6. **Scan view (25.4–39.6) is not justified by voice or picture.** The chartreuse line rises at 25.4, parks at y 0.55
   with "▼ scan view · under the skin" and stays 13 s. Nothing in the voice is about seeing inside, and below the line the
   picture turns into black void: the vein loses its wall and becomes loose red slivers (26.6, 28.6), and from 29.0 the
   whole lower half is black with motes. It hides exactly the depth the line talks about (the returning red ray dives
   into black at 36.5). It is not the bible's scan view (glass cores, glowing edges, iso-depth slices). **Fix:** either
   cut it (keep the slab's cut face, full frame), or bring it in on "Red light sinks in" (33.0) and make it the point: the
   sweep reveals depth slices with the red path reaching the vein (a "~5 mm" depth tick, per c10's 5–10 mm) and the blue
   staying in the top slice; keep the tissue visible under the scan. The tag needs a larger size (see 13).

7. **(c, e) The 75 % readout lands on the wrong sentence.** 27.5–32.9: "seats still full in returning blood, about 75 %"
   appears during "so it's easy to guess the blood inside is too" and "It never is", before "Blood heading home is darker
   red" (30.56). Read with the voice, it seems to back up the wrong idea. **Fix:** start it on "darker" (31.8) and hold it
   through "red light sinks in"; that makes "darker, still three-quarters full" one thought.

8. **(c, glitch) "Arriving: darker" and "leaving: brighter" cells look the same, and readouts overlap at 59.6–60.2.**

9. **(f, g) The last 20 s sit in one place.** 39.6–65.2 is one lung location; 49.7–53.5 is a 3.8 s hold on a cell that
   doesn't change with the counter frozen at 0.25; the takeaway "The blue is your skin's trick" (53.5) plays over lung; the
