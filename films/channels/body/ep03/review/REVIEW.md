## Findings (most important first)

1. **(h) Primitives in every shot.** 0–53 and 60–74: myelin is a row of smooth, identical beige pill capsules, the
   same thing No. 02 round 1 called out (finding 1) and the commit "myelin lamellae" was meant to fix: here no lamellae
   show at any distance. 7.8–9.6: the cell body is a pink sphere with five straight cones (reads as a sea mine).
   14.9–25: the drum is a plain brown cylinder with white speckles on a plain box board; the muscle is a glossy egg;
   the nerve a bent stick. 25.3–53: the bare fibre is a flat pink rod. 54–67: the body is a thin outline icon.
   68–72: the terminal is a sphere on a cylinder beside a large sphere with a Voronoi crackle and a blown highlight.
   **Fix:** myelin as wrapped, slightly irregular sheaths that taper into the node, with the lamellae visible at a cut
   end (one scan-slice cut through the hero sheath would give the scan view its job, see finding 3), the axon visible
   through the node as a darker, wet core; Schwann-cell nuclei bumps on a few sheaths. The cell body as a soft,
   asymmetric soma with tapering, branching dendrites (path.js tubes with radius falloff) and a nucleus seen through
   fresnel translucency. The drum as a period instrument: smoked-paper drum with a soot-scratch trace, brass spindle and
   clockwork, a wooden base with grain and bevel, a muscle with fibre striation hanging from a clamp, the nerve as a
   thin, glistening strand. The bare fibre as a thin wet tube with surface relief. The silhouette as a translucent
   volume (the look test's glass/scan language: rim-lit, iso-depth slices), with the toe-to-brain route as a lit fibre
   inside it. The terminal as a bouton with a textured surface swelling from a tapering stalk, the next cell as a
   membrane with receptors, not a ball; key away from the lens so nothing clips.
