## This episode
- **History.** "For centuries, physicians taught the lung was clotted blood, there to cool the heart" (c4: in Galenic
  medicine the lungs were fleshy, made of coagulated blood) and "Malpighi put an animal's lung under a microscope:
  bubbles" (c5: the vesicles are his first letter's finding; the frog lungs of his second letter gave the
  capillaries). The envelope drawn over the cluster, a dense, dark, smooth mass, is an illustration of the old idea,
  badged.
- **"Up to about twenty-three"** (c2) is Weibel's idealised model; the tree on screen draws 7 forks (badged
  "simplified: 7 of ~23 forks drawn").
- **"Over much of it … under half a micrometre"** (c7): the thin portion, about half the barrier, is under 0.5 µm
  (as thin as about 0.2 µm). The wall is drawn 0.4 µm thick, the wall and the red cell to scale; the O₂ are
  enlarged and their crossing slowed (both badged).
- **"Thicken that wall, and breathing gets harder"** (the stakes): a picture of the idea, not of a particular disease.
- **"About seventy square metres … about a quarter of a tennis court"** (c9, c10): about 70 m² is the commonly cited
  figure; estimates run 50–140 m² (West's textbook gives 50–100 m², morphometry about 130 m²), and the whoa's badge
  says "area to scale · estimates 50–140 m²". The court is a full doubles court (260.8 m²), drawn whole. Its two
  held camera keys were pulled back so the whole court sits inside the 9:16 platform-safe box (x 90–900, y 250–1420;
  projected, not yet seen in a browser). West is cited as the book (9th ed., ch. 1), with no link: check the 50–100 m²
  wording against a library copy. c10's court is from the ITF Rules of Tennis 2026, Rule 1.
- **The tally** runs at 5.5 L a minute (inside the 5–8 L/min resting range, c13), so it reads 6.0 L as "six" is said.
- **The readouts:** 23 forks (c2), 480 million sacs (c1, a mean: 274–790 million), 0.2 mm (c3), 1661 (c5),
  "air–blood wall, under" 0.5 µm (c7), "unfolded, about" 70 m² (c9).
- **The 26 Oct audit** (an independent fact check): lines 3, 4 and 5 re-voiced ("clotted blood", "an animal's lung",
