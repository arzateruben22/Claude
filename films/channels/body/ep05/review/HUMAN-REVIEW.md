## This episode
- **History.** "For centuries, physicians taught the lung was airy flesh, there to cool the heart" (c4, Galen's
  view: light, foam-like flesh, not a solid lung) and Malpighi's 1661 frog lung (c5). The envelope drawn over the
  cluster is an illustration of the old idea, badged.
- **"Up to about twenty-three"** (c2) is Weibel's idealised model; the tree on screen draws 7 forks (badged
  "simplified: 7 of ~23 forks drawn").
- **"At its thinnest … about half a micrometre"** (c7): the wall and the red cell are drawn to scale; the O₂ are
  enlarged and their crossing slowed (both badged).
- **"Thicken that wall, and breathing gets harder"** (the stakes): a picture of the idea, not of a particular disease.
- **"At least seventy square metres … at least a third of a tennis court"** (c9, c10): 70 m² is the low, commonly
  cited figure; morphometry gives about 130 m². The court is a singles court (195.6 m²), drawn whole.
- **The tally** runs at 5.5 L a minute (inside the 5–8 L/min resting range, c13), so it reads 6.0 L as "six" is said.
- **The readouts:** 23 forks (c2), 480 million sacs (c1, a mean: 274–790 million), 0.2 mm (c3), 1661 (c5), 0.5 µm
  (c7), 70 m² (c9).
