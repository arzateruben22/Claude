**Description**

```
Where does gold come from? Look up.

Gold may be leftovers of a star crash: two neutron stars merging make heavy atoms. How much of your gold comes from crashes is still debated.

Series: One More Orbit · Season 2 · Episode 3.

Sources
1. Elements heavier than iron are formed by neutron-capture reactions. — assets.science.nasa.gov: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/astro/programs/cosmic-origins/documents/uv-vis-study-2012/submissions/03.Lawler.pdf
2. Heavy atoms are built by neutron capture. — assets.science.nasa.gov: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/astro/programs/cosmic-origins/documents/uv-vis-study-2012/submissions/03.Lawler.pdf
3. Neutron-star mergers are one place heavy elements form: the 2017 merger GW170817 showed a kilonova powered by freshly made heavy elements (NASA page: 'astronomers think'). — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/unfolding-story-of-kilonova-told-x-rays/
4. GW170817 (Aug 2017): a neutron-star merger seen in gravitational waves, light and X-rays. — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/unfolding-story-of-kilonova-told-x-rays/
5. A neutron star is about 20 km across, with about 1.4 to 2 Suns of mass. — science.nasa.gov: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/stories/quick-reads/whats-inside-a-dead-star/
6. The kilonova glowed from the radioactive decay of freshly made heavy elements. — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/unfolding-story-of-kilonova-told-x-rays/
7. Neutron-star mergers alone may not explain the heavy elements in the Milky Way; collapsars (a kind of massive-star collapse) were proposed as another site, so the share from crashes is debated. — arxiv.org: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03525
8. NASA: astronomers think that after neutron stars merge, the debris makes radioactive elements like platinum and gold, so some gold may come from such crashes; the page says nothing about how much, or about the Sun's gas. — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/unfolding-story-of-kilonova-told-x-rays/
9. High neutron densities lead to the rapid neutron-capture process (r-process). — assets.science.nasa.gov: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/astro/programs/cosmic-origins/documents/uv-vis-study-2012/submissions/03.Lawler.pdf
10. Visible and infrared light from GW170817 was detected several hours after the gravitational waves (telescopes had to search for it). — nasa.gov: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/unfolding-story-of-kilonova-told-x-rays/
11. The gamma-ray burst GRB 170817A was seen +1.74 ± 0.05 s after the gravitational-wave event GW170817. — arxiv.org: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05834
```
