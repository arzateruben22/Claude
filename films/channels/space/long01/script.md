## half (2:15–3:45)
vo: Half the distance. About one hundred ninety-two thousand kilometers. {c6} The Moon now looks twice as wide as it does tonight. {c7} And the ocean has noticed. {-} Tides come from the difference in the Moon's pull across the Earth. {c8} That difference falls off with the cube of the distance. {c9} So half the distance means about eight times the tidal force. {c10} In a thought experiment, the water at your feet would climb the beach. {-} Slowly, in a long swell, hours at a time. {-} We are showing the idea, not a measured height. {-} The Moon is not a calm light any more. {-} It is something that pulls. {-}

## eclipse (4:45–5:45)

## roche (7:30–8:30)

## break (8:30–9:15)

shot: the Moon elongates, fragments, spreads to a glittering arc.
