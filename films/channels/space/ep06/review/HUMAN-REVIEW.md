# What a person must check before this posts

## This episode
- **"At the equator, the ground moves about sixteen hundred seventy kilometers an hour. That beats the speed of
  sound."** True at the equator (1,670 vs 1,225 km/h at sea level and 15 °C); away from it the ground is slower, and
  it beats sound only below about 43° latitude (c1, c2). The line stays tied to the equator.
- **"Since this began, you've gone farther than New York to Boston."** Spoken at 17.4 s: 29.78 km/s × 17.4 s = 517 km
  (> 306 km), relative to the Sun (c4; compile.py asserts it). The spine counts these real km live.
- **Ptolemy** (Almagest I.7, the birds-and-clouds objection, aimed at Earth's spin) is now sourced to G. C. Lewis's
  1862 survey, which quotes it (via a bookseller's excerpt; a public-domain scan is listed by the Wellcome
  Collection), with Toomer's translation as the primary text (c5). **Galileo's cabin** (Dialogue, 1632, Second
  Day) is sourced via Wikipedia (c6, c13); a historian may prefer the primary texts.
- **"You feel a jolt, never a smooth ride."** We don't feel the orbit because Earth falls freely around the Sun; the
  spin can be detected with instruments (Foucault's pendulum, 1851) but not felt (c7's note).
- **230 km/s, 230 million years** are NASA's round figures; modern estimates 220–250 km/s and 225–250 million years
  (c8, c9). "Last time round, the first dinosaurs had just appeared": the earliest known date to about 231–233 million
  years (c10).
- **Readout:** "about a twentieth of a second" counts the galactic leg only, at about 60 s in (c11).
- **Pronunciation at T0:** Ptolemy, Galileo, "sixteen hundred seventy", "two hundred thirty million".

- **After the 26 Oct recheck:** "you" stand on the equator in Ecuador (78.5° W), drawn on the side facing the
  camera; Earth holds still on "Sitting perfectly still" and starts turning (badged 2,000×) on "Earth spins". For
  Ptolemy and Galileo the picture returns to Earth (the orbit clock stops; the spin plays on) with the label "you,
  and the clouds with you"; the one zoom then goes from Earth to the galaxy. The Sun is drawn larger than true (not to
  scale, badged). The readout draws light's path from the Sun to the galaxy's centre.
- **After the 26 Oct script audit:** the why beat's spine heading in the script now reads "what you can feel: a
  jolt, not speed" (Earth's orbital speed itself changes by about 3,600 km/h over a year, unfelt). That heading is
  a script note: the cut's spine shows the km counter, so the picture doesn't change. No line was re-voiced.
