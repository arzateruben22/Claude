# Skin School on TikTok: "Why does a pore clog?" (approved for build; Evelyn reviews before posting)

About 60 s, 9:16, narrated (synthetic voice, labelled as AI), with an original score. Built from
lesson 8-2, "Inside a pore", and its scene. Every claim below is one the lesson already makes
(its four captions). Nothing new is asserted, and the whole script still needs Evelyn's (or a
dermatologist's) sign-off before it posts.

One line per beat. `*starred*` words get a camera landing or a sound hit. `{…}` is direction for
the compiler, not spoken. About 150 words at 2.4–2.6 words a second.

## hook {shot: macroPush · subject: pore mouth · energy: 0.8} (0–4 s)
vo: Every breakout starts in one place: a *single pore*. Let's go inside.

## healthy {shot: craneBack into the cross-section · labels: hair, oil gland, oil} (4–15 s)
vo: This is a healthy pore. A *hair*, an *oil gland* at its base, and oil flowing up and out
onto your skin. As long as the oil keeps moving, the pore stays clear.

## clog {shot: pushIn on the slot · events: plug cells fall and pack} (15–29 s)
vo: Now add *dead skin cells*. They mix with the oil and pack the pore shut. Open to the air,
the plug oxidizes dark: a *blackhead*. Closed over by skin, it stays pale: a *whitehead*.

## inflame {shot: pullBack, then a slow push · events: bacteria appear, the wall swells} (29–43 s)
vo: Trapped oil is food. *Bacteria* feed on it, and the wall swells red: a *papule*. Then,
a *pustule*.

## clear {shot: crane over the drops · events: two liquid drops land and spread} (43–55 s)
vo: So how does it clear? *Mandelic acid* keeps the pore clear. *Benzoyl peroxide* clears
the bacteria.

## time {shot: pull back to the hero wide · events: the counter runs 0 → 12 weeks, 8–12 lit} (53–60 s)
vo: Then give it time. Judge it at *eight to twelve weeks*.
end card: "Part 2: what to put on it." · "Follow Skin School"
