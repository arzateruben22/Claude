# Skin School on TikTok: "Why does a pore clog?" · style guide (draft for approval)

**Film:** 60.0 s · 30 fps · 1,800 frames · 1080×1920 · a 90 BPM grid (one beat = 0.667 s = 20 frames,
a 16th = 5 frames exactly, so every cue lands on a whole frame).
**Client / audience:** Lumevina Skin School. People curious about their skin, scrolling TikTok,
Reels and Shorts, often with the sound off at first.
**The one thing to remember:** *a breakout is a clogged pore. Keep the pore clear, clear the
bacteria, and give it 8 to 12 weeks.*

## 4. Look and motion rules

- **Lens:** 30–55 mm feel. Focus follows what the voice is naming.
- **Motion blur:** 180° shutter. Camera moves land on starred words in the script.
- **Physics:** cells fall with gravity, bounce and squash. Drops stretch as they fall and land
  with a sprung squash and a splash ring.
- **Glow budget:** only the oil, the bacteria and the drops may bloom. Text never blooms.
- **Pacing:** the voice never runs more than 12 s without a picture-only moment (2–4 s) where the
  music comes forward.
- **Safe box (TikTok, Reels and Shorts):** captions, labels and the subject the voice names stay
  inside x 90–900, y 250–1420 at 1080×1920. That keeps the top bar, the right-side buttons and
  the bottom caption area clear on all three. To verify with a private test upload on each app.
- **Frame 0** is a composed close-up of the pore mouth with glowing oil. **The last frame** is the
  end card, so it works as the poster and the loop point.

## 5. Sound: "calm and clean"

| Layer | Choice |
|---|---|
| Key / tempo | D lydian, 90 BPM. Warm and open, no minor turns |
| Bed | Soft pads, felt-piano plucks on the beats, a quiet sub pulse, no drums |
| Labels landing | Small glass bells, rising through the scale shot by shot |
| Science foley | Soft woody taps (cells packing), a faint fizz (bacteria), a warm low swell (the wall), plop and splash (drops), soft ticks (the counter) |
| Space | 1.6 s plate on the music, 0.8 s room on the foley (a small, close world) |
| Voice | af_heart (Kokoro), cleaned and compressed. Music dips 9 dB under it, 80 ms attack, 350 ms release |
| Master | −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −2 dBTP before AAC (≤ −1 after). Separate voice, music and effects tracks, plus music and effects without the voice for a Spanish version |
