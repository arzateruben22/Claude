# What still needs a human

Everything below was either impossible to check from code, or is a judgement that belongs to
a person. The measured gates (framing, focus, readability, motion, sync, loudness, determinism)
are in `review/scorecard.md`.

## Before anyone publishes it

1. **Brand use.** The film uses the Claude name, "Opus 5.5", the coral mascot and the coral
   palette, in the style of a "Made with Claude" announcement. That needs approval from whoever
   owns the brand, or it stays a private study. Nothing in the film says it is an official
   Anthropic piece, but its look implies it.
2. **The editor UI.** The set is a generic compositing editor: a menu bar, project, viewer and
   effects panels, and a layered timeline. Its layout and terms ("Effects & Presets",
   "Composition", the "fx" glyph) echo a well-known commercial app, as the references do. No
   logos, product names or icons from that app are used. Still, have someone check the trade
   dress at a glance.
3. **AI-content labelling.** Platforms that ask whether content is AI-generated will want an
   answer. Every frame and sound is generated by code written with an AI assistant. No
   generative image, video or voice model was used.
4. **Flashing.** The pop at 1.97 s, the impact at 2.0 s and the four zaps (6.5–9.0 s) are brief
   bright events, at most two inside any one second. That should pass the usual three-flashes-
   per-second guideline, but it hasn't been run through a photosensitivity analyzer. Do that
   before any broadcast use.

## Taste calls, worth a look on a real screen

5. **Listen to it.** The score and foley were judged only by measurement: loudness −14 LUFS
   integrated and −1 dBTP true peak after normalization, the spectral balance per shot, and the
   cue sync. Nobody has heard it. Check it on phone speakers and on headphones, especially the
   impact at 2.0 s, the glass, and the end bells.
6. **The 9:16 end card** now stacks MADE WITH / CLAUDE so it can be framed closer. The 16:9 cut
   keeps the reference's single line. Decide whether the portrait lockup is acceptable for the
   brand.
7. **S5, the keyframe run.** The camera now pulls back so the title builds in the background
   while the mascot trots and zaps. That's what the shot list asked for, but it trades away the
   big hero close-ups of pass 1.
8. **Character acting.** The stomp now hangs, then slams. Keystrokes bounce the mascot, and it
   leans back to look up at the lens on the portrait end card. These are acting choices.

## Known limits, not defects

- Depth of field uses the depth at the centre of the shutter, not one per sub-frame. Its edges
  can be slightly too sharp against heavy motion blur. This shows on whips only, never on holds.
- The renderer is SwiftShader (CPU WebGL), so a full render takes hours. The look is identical
  on a GPU and much faster there, but bit-exact determinism has only been verified on
  SwiftShader.
